## Your route to the Greece Golden Visa

### A change-of-use property

The project is a hotel converted into apartments.

### Investment from €250,000

The change-of-use model is what places the project at the reduced €250,000 Greece Golden Visa investment level.

### A 5-year residence permit

The permit runs for 5 years and is renewable in 5-year periods for as long as you continue to own the qualifying property.

The Golden Visa is a Greek residence permit, not EU citizenship; the right to live freely in other EU countries arises only once you naturalize.

### The route to Greek citizenship

#### A route to citizenship

Once you have completed the period of lawful residence required by Greek law, you can look at the route to Greek citizenship.

#### A separate step

Naturalization is a separate step, with its own conditions on physical residence, language, integration, and official assessment.

#### EU citizenship rights

EU citizenship rights, including the right to live and work in EU member states, arise only after naturalization is granted.
