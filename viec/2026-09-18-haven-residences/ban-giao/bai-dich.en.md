## Structural features designed to manage investment risk

#### Significant developer equity

- Developer equity accounts for more than 50% of total project capital.
- The developer has substantial capital at risk alongside EB-5 investors, so the risk does not rest solely on them.

#### Limited reliance on EB-5 financing

- Total capitalization of US$132 million, with more than 50% equity
- Parkview is providing a US$64 million construction loan (including a US$1 million bonus upon project completion)
- EB-5 capital of up to US$24 million, only a small share of the total
- If EB-5 capital is not fully raised, Parkview can increase its loan disbursements to cover the shortfall
- Significantly reduces dependence on raising the full EB-5 amount

#### Where EB-5 capital sits in the capital stack

- EB-5 capital is structured as a participation in the Parkview loan
- EB-5 capital is secured by a first-position lien on the entire project
- If the developer becomes insolvent, Parkview, as the lenders' representative, handles disposal of the collateral and distributes the proceeds to the EB-5 fund pro rata to its contribution

#### Collateral value relative to debt

- Appraised stabilized value: US$158.6 million (per an independent appraisal report)
- Maximum total loan balance: US$64 million
- A substantial cushion between appraised value and debt, supporting a refinancing or sale if needed

#### Personal guarantees

- The developer has guaranteed completion of the project
- The developer has provided a personal guarantee of loan repayment, backed by the developer's entire personal assets
