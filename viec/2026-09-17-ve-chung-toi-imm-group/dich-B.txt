[s069] 2000–2022
[s070] The entrepreneurial journey
[s071|#viet-nam-hop-le] Born into a poor family in the Mekong Delta of southern Vietnam, Mr. Tinh set out to build his career in Saigon in 2000. In 2005, after two unsuccessful ventures, he founded the first immigration consultancy in Vietnam. This third venture laid the foundation for today's IMM Group.
[s072] Through the ups and downs and the hard lessons of practice, he has continued to learn through books, global partnerships, and short courses from Harvard University, including Leadership Principles, Management Essentials, and Sustainable Business Strategy.
[s073] In 2022, wishing to devote himself to charitable work, Mr. Tinh handed over most of the company's day-to-day operations to the board of directors and formally established the Be Better Foundation, which helps disadvantaged children stay in school and pursue a university education.
[s074] Published books
[s075] In 2024, he published “[Sống trọn vẹn một đời đáng sống](https://bebetter.org.vn/song-tron-ven-mot-doi-dang-song/)” (Living a Life Worth Living to the Fullest), which shares lessons from his life's journey. The book's message is that anyone can achieve a meaningful, balanced, and successful life by choosing to live each day fully.
[s076] More recently, he published “[Thành công trong cuộc sống – Thành nhân giữa đường đời](https://bebetter.org.vn/product/thanh-cong-trong-cuoc-song-thanh-nhan-giua-duong-doi/)” (Success in Life – Becoming a Person of Character Along the Way). The book is a candid account from a father after more than 20 years in business: a journey through the stumbles of greed and mistakes to finding peace in core life values. He believes that love, sharing, gratitude, and resilience are values everyone needs, and that belief moved him to share these reflections more widely.

[s079] Why we work in this field
[s080|#viet-nam-hop-le] The story of IMM Group's founder is the conviction behind our work in this field. Although he came from a humble position in society, in a country like Vietnam that is still working to reduce poverty, Mr. Tran Van Tinh (Tony Tinh) believes that Vietnamese people are not inferior or “lesser,” as some racist views around the world hold. Vietnamese people can achieve great things just like any other people in the world, and they deserve a good, civilized life like that in developed countries.
[s081|#viet-nam: tiếp nối niềm tin của nhà sáng lập về người Việt ở s080, bỏ Việt Nam thì câu mất nghĩa] We believe that successful Vietnamese entrepreneurs should have free access to new opportunities around the world. Reaching out to the world will raise the standing of Vietnamese people in the international community and change the future of generations to come.
[s082] Entrepreneurs are now seen as a frontline force in the 21st century because they contribute the most to their communities, both financially and in creating opportunities. It is freedom that has enabled them to achieve ever greater success and lead the world's progress. This freedom takes 4 forms:
[s083] 01Freedom of global mobility
[s084] A successful entrepreneur deserves the freedom to travel the world. Whether traveling for business or vacationing with family, they can choose the time that suits them best without being held back by visa procedures.
[s085] 02Freedom of global finance
[s086] Successful people need the tools to diversify their investments across borders and allocate their wealth prudently for their children and grandchildren. At the same time, their privacy must be fully protected.
[s087] 03Freedom to access global knowledge and education
[s088] It is not only entrepreneurs who should have broader opportunities to learn around the world. The next generation also needs access to the world's most advanced education so they can freely reach their full potential.
[s089] 04Freedom to shape one's life
[s090] The ability to act on opportunities gives successful people the freedom to choose where they live. And when they can work and do business in a society that matches their preferences and aspirations, they can grow personally and professionally in their own way.
