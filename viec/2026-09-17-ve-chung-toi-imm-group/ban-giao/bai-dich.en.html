Serving with Authentic Values

Guided by our principle, "Serving with Authentic Values", IMM Group recognizes its responsibility to uphold integrity, a positive outlook, and genuine dedication in everything the company does. Our advisory process includes multiple layers of assessment of each investor's suitability and likelihood of a successful application before any contract is signed with IMM Group. Our advice is always thorough and prudent, so that clients have the best possible chance of success. Mission and business philosophy

Our mission

IMM Group focuses on building a sustainable business, developing the quality and value of its people, and making a practical contribution to the community. We have defined 3 purposes that guide our work:

01

To remain the most trusted investment migration advisory firm in Vietnam

Founded in January 2005, IMM Group is a pioneer with the longest track record in Vietnam in advising on investment-based permanent residence and citizenship abroad. Our foremost purpose is to be the country's most trusted investment migration advisory firm.

We have a clear direction and consistently follow the operating principles, professional ethics and core values set by our founder. Through many years of ups and downs in the immigration field, we know what must be done to minimize risk for our clients, and in every circumstance we do what is right and best for them.

02

Sustainable growth

Sustainable growth, as we define it, rests on 3 factors: financial sustainability, sustainability of our people, and sustainable quality of products and services.

Sustainable growth must rest on lasting foundations: sound business values, including integrity, sincerity, professional ethics and service quality. To protect the quality of our service, we never chase short-term opportunities or quick profits.

Sustainable growth also means building a team of people with strong values. We believe that people with strong values will always work with dedication and attend to every detail to protect our clients' interests.

03

Building a positive community that lives with purpose and meaning

At IMM Group, we understand that each member's growth comes from a balance between good personal qualities and the ethical principles of society. We give every individual in the company the opportunity to grow in their career while finding a meaningful purpose and living a happy, positive life.

Through the growth of each individual and of the group as a whole, the IMM Group team believes that together we can make a positive impact on society. Each person in the company can become successful and, in turn, contribute to the development of society and the next generation. The principle of exponential influence allows this positive effect to grow stronger over time.

Joy and happiness cannot truly exist if they are merely personal, petty, and self-centered. Nor can they last without contributing to and sharing with others. With this in mind, IMM Group and its members are committed to supporting the community with financial resources, experience, and knowledge, to help build a better life, a better society, and a better world. Through the Be Better Foundation, the charity we established, we hope to change the lives and futures of many disadvantaged children, and that is one of the reasons we exist.

IMM Group's guiding principle

Serving with Authentic Values

Serving with Authentic Values is our guiding principle. Nothing is absolute or 100% perfect. However, grounded in the company's origins, its cultural values, and the mission set by its founder, IMM Group is committed to delivering services across the entire group based on genuine and principled values.

Serving with Authentic Values means:

For clients:

  • The entire Board of Directors and all staff share the understanding that we provide services as a business, but always with sincerity and honesty, never on the basis that money comes first.
  • We do not distort the truth to take advantage of clients.
  • We do not use vague, exaggerated or misleading advertising to attract clients.
  • We do not exploit clients' difficulties, circumstances or needs for improper gain.
  • We do not treat clients as a business tool; we treat them as benefactors and friends, and seek to understand, empathize with, and support them.
  • We do not pressure, lure or push clients into using our services through dishonest tactics.
  • When we make a mistake, we honestly acknowledge it and apologize to the client, and we provide a reasonable solution that is fair and appropriate for the client.
  • We do not use clients' information or images for marketing, directly or indirectly, and we always strictly protect clients' confidential and private information.
  • Where possible, we prioritize helping clients in urgent or difficult situations rather than adhering to a rigid, inflexible service process.
  • We discuss the relevant risks and opportunities openly with clients and tell them clearly and honestly about the likelihood of failure or success of their case.

For partners:

  • We choose partners based on integrity, prudence, sustainability, and honesty rather than on outward image and brand alone.
  • We research and assess partners thoroughly, and never overlook risk factors for clients for the sake of revenue or high profits.
  • We decline to work with a partner whose practices we consider to pose a high risk to clients.
  • We decline to work with a partner whose product lacks full legal standing or is based on untruthful information, carrying significant potential risk for clients.

Business philosophy

IMM Group's business philosophy is summed up in this statement by our CEO and founder, Tran Van Tinh (Tony Tinh): "Good people have good values and ethics. A good business has good people and good partners, and serves good clients." This message guides every aspect of the group, from core values, culture, and mission to our people and our internal and external relationships.

Good people show kindness and care, generosity in spirit as well as in action, and genuine consideration for others. They typically stand out for qualities such as honesty, humility, fairness, responsibility and empathy. These values help them build credibility and form relationships with people who share the same ethical foundation, which in turn opens up more opportunities in life.

IMM Group is committed to building a team that embodies strong ethical qualities, and makes these the foundation for the sustainable growth of the entire group. We earn the trust of clients and partners through core values such as candor, transparency and a positive attitude, and by always understanding, supporting and standing alongside them in every situation.

Every department of IMM Group operates in compliance with the law and places honesty and sincerity at the center of every transaction. With clients, we listen carefully and treat them like family, so that we can provide the services that serve them best. With partners, we build sincere, mutually beneficial relationships that help maximize value for our clients. And with the community, we are always mindful of contributing to the broader development of society.

The IMM Group story

Founder of IMM Group

Tran Van Tinh

Tony Tinh

Mr. Tran Van Tinh (Tony Tinh), founder of IMM Group, has led the group with a mission to build lasting values, culture, and operating principles. Under his leadership, IMM Group has become more than a business: it stands for dedication and commitment to the community.

2000–2022

The entrepreneurial journey

Born into a poor family in the Mekong Delta of southern Vietnam, Mr. Tinh set out to build his career in Saigon in 2000. In 2005, after two unsuccessful ventures, he founded the first immigration consultancy in Vietnam. This third venture laid the foundation for today's IMM Group.

Through the ups and downs and the hard lessons of practice, he has continued to learn through books, global partnerships, and short courses from Harvard University, including Leadership Principles, Management Essentials, and Sustainable Business Strategy.

In 2022, wishing to devote himself to charitable work, Mr. Tinh handed over most of the company's day-to-day operations to the board of directors and formally established the Be Better Foundation, which helps disadvantaged children stay in school and pursue a university education.

Published books

In 2024, he published "Sống trọn vẹn một đời đáng sống" (Living a Life Worth Living to the Fullest), which shares lessons from his life's journey. The book's message is that anyone can achieve a meaningful, balanced, and successful life by choosing to live each day fully.

More recently, he published "Thành công trong cuộc sống – Thành nhân giữa đường đời" (Success in Life – Becoming a Person of Character Along the Way). The book is a candid account from a father after more than 20 years in business: a journey through the stumbles of greed and mistakes to finding peace in core life values. He believes that love, sharing, gratitude, and resilience are values everyone needs, and that belief moved him to share these reflections more widely.

Learn more about Mr. Tran Van Tinh on his personal website, tonytinh.me, and read his reflections at facebook.com/tonytinh.

Why we work in this field

The story of IMM Group's founder is the conviction behind our work in this field. Although he came from a humble position in society, in a country like Vietnam that is still working to reduce poverty, Mr. Tran Van Tinh (Tony Tinh) believes that Vietnamese people are not inferior or "lesser," as some racist views around the world hold. Vietnamese people can achieve great things just like any other people in the world, and they deserve a quality of life comparable to that in developed countries.

We believe that successful Vietnamese entrepreneurs should have free access to new opportunities around the world. Reaching out to the world will raise the standing of Vietnamese people in the international community and change the future of generations to come.

Entrepreneurs are now seen as being at the forefront of the 21st century because they contribute the most to their communities, both financially and in creating opportunities. It is freedom that has enabled them to achieve ever greater success and lead the world's progress. This freedom takes 4 forms:

01. Freedom of global mobility

A successful entrepreneur deserves the freedom to travel the world. Whether traveling for business or vacationing with family, they can choose the time that suits them best without being held back by visa procedures.

02. Freedom of global finance

Successful people need the tools to diversify their investments across borders and allocate their wealth prudently for their children and grandchildren. At the same time, their privacy must be fully protected.

03. Freedom to access global knowledge and education

It is not only entrepreneurs who should have broader opportunities to learn around the world. The next generation also needs access to advanced education internationally so they can freely reach their full potential.

04. Freedom to shape one's life

The ability to act on opportunities gives successful people the freedom to choose where they live. And when they can work and do business in a society that matches their preferences and aspirations, they can grow personally and professionally in their own way.
