[s037] Serving with Authentic Values, IMM Group’s guiding principle
[s038] IMM Group’s guiding principle (slogan)
[s039] Serving with Authentic Values
[s040] **Serving with Authentic Values**, in Vietnamese “**Phục vụ bằng Giá trị Chân chính**”. Nothing is absolute or 100% perfect. However, grounded in the company’s origins, its cultural values and the mission set by its founder, **IMM Group** is committed to, and directs the entire group toward, delivering services based on genuine and principled values.
[s041] **Serving with Authentic Values** means:
[s042] **For clients:**
[s043] The entire Board of Directors and all staff share the understanding that we provide services as a business, but always with sincerity and honesty, never on the basis that money comes first.
[s044] We do not distort the truth to take advantage of clients.
[s045] We do not use vague, exaggerated or misleading advertising to attract clients.
[s046] We do not exploit clients’ difficulties, circumstances or needs for improper gain.
[s047] We do not treat clients as a business tool; we treat them as benefactors and friends, so that we can understand, share with and support them.
[s048] We do not pressure, lure or push clients into using our services through dishonest tactics.
[s049] When we make a mistake, we honestly acknowledge it and apologize to the client, and we provide a reasonable solution that is fair and appropriate for the client.
[s050] We do not use clients’ information or images for marketing, directly or indirectly, and we always strictly protect clients’ confidential and private information.
[s051] Where possible, we prioritize helping clients in urgent or difficult situations rather than adhering to a rigid, inflexible service process.
[s052] We discuss the relevant risks and opportunities openly with clients and tell them clearly and honestly about the likelihood of failure or success of their case.
[s053] **For partners:**
[s054] We choose partners based on sound, secure, sustainable and honest values rather than on outward image and brand alone.
[s055] We research and assess partners thoroughly, and never overlook risk factors for clients for the sake of revenue or high profits.
[s056] We decline to work with a partner whose practices we consider to pose a high risk to clients.
[s057] We decline to work with a partner whose product lacks full legal standing or is based on untruthful information, carrying significant potential risk for clients.

A good business has good people and good partners, and serves good clients**”. This message guides every aspect of the group, from core values, culture and mission to our people and our internal and external relationships.
[s060] Good people show kindness and care, generosity in spirit as well as in action, and genuine consideration for others. They typically stand out for qualities such as honesty, humility, fairness, responsibility and empathy. These values help them build credibility and form relationships with people who share the same ethical foundation, which in turn opens up more opportunities in life.
[s061] IMM Group is committed to building a team that embodies strong ethical qualities, and makes these the foundation for the sustainable growth of the entire group. We earn the trust of clients and partners through core values such as candor, transparency and a positive attitude, and by always understanding, supporting and standing alongside them in every situation.
[s062] Every department of IMM Group operates in compliance with the law and places honesty and sincerity at the center of every transaction. With clients, we listen carefully and treat them like family, so that we can provide the services that serve them best. With partners, we build sincere, mutually beneficial relationships that help maximize value for our clients. And with the community, we are always mindful of contributing to the broader development of society.
