## An overview of European residency by investment

What is European residency by investment?

- Obtain residency, a European Golden Visa, or European citizenship through investment
- Your family can live, study, and do business in the country that runs the program
- Many programs allow travel within the Schengen Area under the applicable rules
- You can work toward European citizenship if you meet the residence, language, and integration conditions

Invest for a residence permit or European Golden Visa, or for European citizenship. The 2 most common routes are real estate and investing in or donating to a fund.

Ireland Golden Visa

Investment

From €400,000

Purpose

Irish residency

a route to EU citizenship, with special rights in the UK

Processing

8–10

months

How you invest

- A €400,000 endowment to a qualifying social project
- Or a €1,000,000 investment in a qualifying business or project, held for 3 years

Key benefits

You transfer the funds only after pre-approval

Keep Stamp 4 status with a minimal residence requirement of 1 day a year

After 5 years of residence in Ireland, you can apply for naturalization

With Irish citizenship, you can live and work freely across the EU and in the United Kingdom

Points to consider

Ireland's Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP) stopped accepting new applications on February 15, 2023

Only endowment places from projects that qualified before that date remain

Malta Golden Visa

Investment

From €375,000

minimum

Purpose

Maltese residency

+ free travel in the 29 Schengen countries

Processing

About 8–10

months

How you invest

- Buy property worth at least €375,000, or lease property for at least €14,000 a year
- Hold the property or the lease for at least 5 years
- A government contribution, administrative fees, and a charitable donation, as required

Key benefits

Permanent residence in Malta

Travel across the 29 Schengen countries for up to 90 days

A multigenerational family can be included in one application

No minimum stay is required to keep your permanent residence

Please note

After 5 years you may sell the property or end the lease without affecting your permanent residence

Citizenship requires meeting the residence conditions set by Maltese law

05 Group A · Option 05

Portugal Golden Visa

Investment

From €500,000

minimum

Purpose

Portuguese residency

Schengen travel, a route to EU citizenship

Processing

12–16

months

How you invest (choose 1)

- An investment fund: €500,000 or more, held for at least 5 years, with 60% invested in Portuguese commercial companies
- Scientific research: €500,000 or more
- A cultural donation: €250,000 or more, non-refundable
- Forming a commercial company: €500,000 or more plus 5 jobs; or forming a new company plus 10 jobs
- Real estate: €280,000 or more — closed since October 7, 2023

Key benefits

Your whole family can live, work, and study in Portugal

Travel across the 29 Schengen countries under the short-stay rules

You can apply for citizenship after 5 years if you meet the conditions

With citizenship you can live in EU countries, and you may be able to explore the US E-2 route

Please note

You need to spend just 14 days in Portugal every 2 years to keep your Golden Visa

The fund route is now the main option, since Portugal has restricted the real estate route for the Golden Visa
