[s003] What is the Guest Investor Program?
[s004] The Hungary Golden Visa, officially the Guest Investor Program, is an investor residence program created by the Hungarian government under Act XC of 2023.
[s005] It is open to investors from outside the EU, and to their families, through a qualifying investment.
[s006] The residence permit runs for up to 10 years and can be renewed for up to 10 more.
[s007] Short stays in the Schengen Area under the 90/180-day rule.
[s008] Two investment routes are in force, since July 1, 2024:
[s009] From €250,000 into a real estate fund registered with the National Bank of Hungary (MNB), or
[s010] A donation from €1,000,000 to a public-interest higher education institution.

[s073|#bo-qua-tn: nhãn mục hỏi đáp dùng tên sản phẩm, khớp H1] Hungary Golden Visa FAQs
[s074] What is the minimum investment?
[s075] The minimum investment
[s076] There are 2 routes: from €250,000 into a real estate fund registered with the MNB, held for at least 5 years, or a donation from €1,000,000 to a public-interest higher education institution. The amounts and conditions that apply are those in force when you file.
[s077] Can I work in Hungary?
[s078] Working in Hungary
[s079] Yes. The investor residence permit allows you to work, be self-employed, or run a business in Hungary without restriction — an advantage over many other European golden visa programs.
[s080] How many days do I have to spend in Hungary?
[s081] Days you have to spend in Hungary
[s082] There is no minimum stay requirement to hold or renew the investor residence permit. If you are aiming at citizenship later, you will need to meet the residence and integration conditions set by Hungarian law.
[s083] How long is the permit valid?
[s084] Validity of the permit
[s085] The residence permit runs for up to 10 years and can be renewed for up to 10 more. On the fund route you keep the investment to renew; on the donation route no further investment is required.
[s086] What is the real estate fund, and what are the risks?
[s087] The real estate fund and its risks
[s088] It is a real estate fund registered with the National Bank of Hungary (MNB) and run by a licensed fund manager; at least 40% of its net asset value is residential property in Hungary. This is an investment that carries risk, and the outcome and the recovery of your capital depend on your file, on the fund, and on how the market moves.
[s089] Who counts as family?
[s090] Family members you can include
[s091] The application can include your spouse and minor children; certain other dependents may join through family reunification. The exact conditions follow the OIF rules — contact IMM Group to check your own family.
[s092] How do I document my source of funds?
[s093] Documenting your source of funds
[s094] You need to document a lawful source for the investment capital. Every family documents this differently — income, a business, the sale of an asset. Contact IMM Group for an assessment of your own source of funds.
[s095] When can I apply for citizenship?
[s096] When you can apply for citizenship
[s097] Under Hungarian law, naturalization generally requires around 8 years of continuous residence, along with language, integration, and means-of-support conditions. The investor residence permit can be a first step; the actual timeline and conditions depend on your file.
