[s003] What is the L-1A visa?
[s004] The L-1A visa is a U.S. nonimmigrant work visa for managers and senior executives transferring from a company abroad to a related company in the U.S.
[s005] Companies can use the L-1A visa to send leaders to run a subsidiary, branch, parent company, or affiliate in the U.S.
[s006] Your spouse and children under 21 can accompany you on L-2 dependent visas.
[s007] After running the business in the U.S. and meeting the requirements, the investor may pursue an EB-1C green card.

[s081] L-1A visa FAQs
[s082] Is the L-1A an immigrant visa?
[s083] What L-1A is
[s084] No. The L-1A is a temporary, nonimmigrant work visa. It allows managers and senior executives to transfer to the U.S. to work for a related company.
[s085] Can the L-1A visa lead to a green card?
[s086] The EB-1C route
[s087] Yes, potentially. Once the U.S. business is operating and meets the requirements, the investor may file an EB-1C petition for a green card.
[s088] Does EB-1C require a US$800,000 investment like EB-5?
[s089] Investment amount
[s090] No. EB-1C does not require a fixed investment amount as EB-5 does, but you must meet the requirements for the business, the managerial position, and business operations.
[s091] Can my family come with me?
[s092] Family members
[s093] Yes. Your spouse and children under 21 can accompany you in L-2 status.
[s094] Can my children attend school in the U.S.?
[s095] Education rights
[s096] Yes. Children in L-2 status can attend public schools while in valid status.
[s097] How long does processing take?
[s098] Processing time
[s099] Processing takes about 3 months as a guide. USCIS premium processing is available, but actual timing still depends on your case.
[s100] Who is the L-1A visa for?
[s101] Who it suits
[s102] Business owners and senior executives outside the U.S. who want to expand into the U.S. and run the U.S. company themselves.
[s103] What are the main risks of the L-1A visa?
[s104] Key risks
[s105] The main risk lies in proving that the business is genuine, the managerial role is real, the business plan is viable, and you can run the U.S. company effectively.
