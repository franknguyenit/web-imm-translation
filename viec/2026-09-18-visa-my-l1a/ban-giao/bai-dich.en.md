# U.S. L-1A Visa for Intracompany Executives and Managers

### What is the L-1A visa?

- The L-1A visa is a U.S. nonimmigrant work visa for managers and senior executives transferring from a company abroad to a related company in the U.S.
- Companies can use the L-1A visa to send leaders to run a subsidiary, branch, parent company, or affiliate in the U.S.
- Your spouse and children under 21 can accompany you on L-2 dependent visas.
- After running the business in the U.S. and meeting the requirements, you may pursue an EB-1C green card.

## L-1A visa benefits

### Live and work in the U.S.

- You can work in the U.S. and manage and run the company for as long as your L-1A status remains valid.

### Your family comes with you

- Your spouse and children under 21 can accompany you in L-2 status.

### U.S. schooling for your children

- Dependent children can attend public schools while in valid status.

### Hands-on control of your business

- You directly manage the U.S. subsidiary under the business model you have established.

### A path to an EB-1C green card

- If the company and your managerial role meet the requirements, you may file for an EB-1C green card.

## Advantages of the L-1A visa

### Fast processing

- Processing typically takes around 3 months and can be shortened with premium processing by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). Actual timing depends on your case.

### A path to a green card

- The L-1A visa can open a path to an EB-1C green card if the business and your managerial role meet the requirements.

### No fixed investment amount

- Unlike EB-5, EB-1C does not require a fixed investment amount, but you must meet the business and managerial-role requirements.

### Your family moves with you

- Your spouse and children under 21 can accompany you, living and studying in the U.S. in the appropriate visa status.

### Suited to business owners with an established company outside the U.S.

- Designed for business owners and senior executives who want to expand their business into the U.S.

### Direct control of your business

- You are directly involved in running the U.S. company; business results depend on your operating capability and market conditions.

## L-1A visa requirements

### Company requirements

- The parent company outside the U.S. has the scale and financial capacity to invest in the U.S.
- The U.S. company is a subsidiary, branch, parent company, or affiliate of the company abroad.
- The U.S. company has a dedicated office.
- There is a viable business plan for the U.S.
- The U.S. company is large enough to need a manager or senior executive.

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older.
- Currently holds a managerial or senior executive position at the parent company abroad.
- Has the capability and experience to manage and run the U.S. company.
- Meets background security and medical requirements.

### Documentation requirements

- Evidence of the qualifying relationship between the company abroad and the U.S. company.
- Evidence of the applicant's managerial or executive role.
- Evidence that the U.S. business can genuinely operate and needs a senior leader.

## L-1A visa FAQs

#### Is the L-1A an immigrant visa?

What L-1A is

- No. The L-1A is a temporary, nonimmigrant work visa. It allows managers and senior executives to transfer to the U.S. to work for a related company.

#### Can the L-1A visa lead to a green card?

The EB-1C route

- Yes, potentially. Once the U.S. business is operating and meets the requirements, you may file an EB-1C petition for a green card.

#### Does EB-1C require a US$800,000 investment like EB-5?

Investment amount

- No. Unlike EB-5, EB-1C does not require a fixed investment amount, but you must meet the requirements for the business, the managerial position, and business operations.

#### Can my family come with me?

Family members

- Yes. Your spouse and children under 21 can accompany you in L-2 status.

#### Can my children attend school in the U.S.?

Schooling

- Yes. Children in L-2 status can attend public schools while in valid status.

#### How long does processing take?

Processing time

- Processing typically takes around 3 months. USCIS premium processing is available, but actual timing still depends on your case.

#### Who is the L-1A visa for?

Who it suits

- Business owners and senior executives outside the U.S. who want to expand into the U.S. and run the U.S. company themselves.

#### What are the main risks of the L-1A visa?

Key risks

- The main risk lies in proving that the business is genuine, the managerial role is real, the business plan is viable, and you can run the U.S. company effectively.
