[s008] Deciding early brings real financial benefits, but the decision should never be driven by emotion
[s009] One clear pattern in the history of the EB-5 program is that the minimum investment amount has risen over time (from US$500,000 to US$800,000), and it is expected to rise further. That is why timing matters so much.
[s010] We always advise clients not to rush into committing capital out of a passing emotion, a fear of missing out, or pressure from aggressive marketing. That said, if your family has done its research, understands its needs, and is confident the program is the right fit, deciding early can bring substantial financial benefits. Acting sooner lets you lock in today's lower investment amount and can save you a considerable sum compared with waiting a few years and then having to invest a much higher amount.
[s011] So, is EB-5 worth it?
[s012] For a family with solid finances, capital that can be committed for the long term, clear goals for their children, and a desire to build an international foothold, EB-5 can be well worth it. Its value reaches far beyond a green card. It lies in your children's future, in greater flexibility for your business, in not concentrating all your risk in one place, and in the freedom for each family member to choose the life they want.
[s013] A responsible advisory firm should not try to convince every family that America is the right choice. Its proper role is to help each family understand clearly what they will gain, what they will give up, and how they need to prepare.
