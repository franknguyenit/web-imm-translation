## Domain notes for client

1. **Minimum investment (s009).** Under the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022, the minimum is US$1,050,000, or US$800,000 for a TEA project. The historical figures were US$1,000,000 and US$500,000. The sentence as written compares only the TEA amounts. Suggest the client confirm whether to say so explicitly.
2. **Future increases (s009, s010).** The 2022 law says the amounts are adjusted for inflation (CPI-U) starting January 1, 2027, then every 5 years. So "expected to rise" is well founded. But "a much higher amount" (source: "cao hơn nhiều") depends on inflation and is not certain. Client to confirm the date and wording with counsel before publishing, since the first adjustment is only about 3.5 months away.
3. **"Lock in" (s010).** The amount that applies is generally set when the I-526/I-526E petition is filed, not when a family decides. The source's "lock in" idea is fair only in that sense. The wording was softened to "can allow". Client to consider whether to mention filing.
4. **Capital at risk (s004).** This section frames the worst case as "no return". EB-5 rules require the capital to be at risk, including possible loss of principal. The disclaimer (s018) does not say this. We recommend an at-risk sentence somewhere in the full article if it is not already in questions 1–4 or earlier sections.
5. **Disclaimer (s018).** It is faithful and adequate. Optionally, the client could name "an independent immigration attorney" (style guide §3), but this adds wording that is not in the source, so it is left to the client.
