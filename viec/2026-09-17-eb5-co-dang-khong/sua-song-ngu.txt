[s002] If your family cannot move to the U.S. at the same time, how will you manage your work and the care of parents on both sides?
[s008] Deciding early brings significant financial benefits, but the decision should not be driven by emotion.
[s012] For a family with solid finances, capital that can be committed for the long term, clear goals for their children, and a desire to build an international foothold, EB-5 can be well worth it. Its value reaches far beyond a green card. It lies in your children's future, in greater control over your business, in not concentrating all your risk in one place, and in the freedom for each family member to choose the life they want.
