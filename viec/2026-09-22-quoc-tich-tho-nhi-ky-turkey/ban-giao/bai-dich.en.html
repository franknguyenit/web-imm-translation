Turkey Citizenship by Investment

What is Turkey citizenship by investment?

  • Turkey citizenship by investment is a program established by the Government of the Republic of Türkiye.
  • You and your family can become Turkish citizens once you meet the investment requirement.
  • Investment level: from US$400,000.
  • Once you hold citizenship, you have visa-free or eVisa access to more than 110 countries.
  • Turkish citizenship opens a route to the US E-2 treaty investor visa, to invest and live in the United States.
  • To qualify for the E-2 visa: 3 continuous years of residence and ties in Türkiye before you file, a condition US law applies to citizenship acquired through investment.

Turkey citizenship by investment FAQs

What are the investment risks?

Risks to keep in mind

  • The main risks sit with the real estate project itself.
  • For a property to qualify, an independent valuation must put its value at US$400,000 or more, it must never have been used for a citizenship application before, and it must not currently be owned by a foreign national.
  • On the citizenship side, risk is reduced when the file follows the process exactly: the program has no medical examination, no declaration of assets, and no restriction based on your current nationality.
  • IMM Group prepares the file to the standard the government requires.

Note: real estate remains an investment, and the value of the asset can rise or fall.

How is the investment structured?

A qualifying real estate purchase

  • For Turkey citizenship by investment you purchase qualifying real estate valued at US$400,000 or more, exclusive of taxes and fees.
  • After roughly 12–18 months of processing, depending on your file and on the authorities' own timelines, you, your spouse, and your unmarried, financially dependent children under 18 are granted Turkish citizenship.

How long must the property be held?

The holding period

  • Under the rules you hold the property for 3 years and give an undertaking not to sell before then; that undertaking is recorded on the Turkish land registry system.
  • During the holding period there is nothing to renew and no periodic filing to make.
  • After 3 years, IMM Group and our partners can help you resell the property without affecting your citizenship; family or friends may also buy it from you, subject to the eligibility rules that apply to their own application.
  • If you would rather keep the asset and rent it out, you can sign a rental agreement with the developer.

What happens to my citizenship if the project fails?

Citizenship and project risk are two separate things

  • Once granted, citizenship is held for life and is not, in itself, affected by the commercial failure of the real estate project: where the file met the requirements and the investment went into a qualifying project, you keep the citizenship you were granted.
  • The distinction matters, though: the property remains an investment that carries risk to both its value and the return of your capital — two separate questions.

What makes a property eligible?

Under the rules updated on March 22, 2021

  • Each title deed can be used for a citizenship application only once.
  • The property must be bought from an individual or company of a nationality other than your own — in practice, from a Turkish seller.

The current threshold is US$400,000 or more, in force since the 2022 update. The rules that apply are those in force when you file.

What public benefits do citizens receive?

Education and healthcare

  • With Turkish citizenship, your children attend public schools free of charge.
  • The whole family receives free care at public hospitals and substantially reduced fees at private ones; Türkiye is among the region's leading destinations for medical travel.

Which countries can I travel to visa-free?

Travel on a Turkish passport

  • Visa-free or eVisa access to more than 110 countries, including Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, and Singapore.
  • A second passport can also make visa applications easier; the outcome in any given country, including the EU and the Schengen Area, still depends on that country's own rules and decision.

Note: Türkiye is not part of the Schengen Area.

What language is spoken in Türkiye?

Language in Türkiye

  • The official language is Turkish, and English is widely used in many places.
  • The citizenship by investment application has no language requirement.

What is the economy like?

Türkiye's economy at a glance

  • A large G20 economy and a trading gateway between Europe and Asia, with strengths in tourism, energy, and automotive manufacturing — a practical base for expanding an international business.
  • Türkiye is among the world's leading tourist destinations, drawing tens of millions of visitors a year.

What is the education system like?

Higher education

  • Türkiye has more than 170 universities and draws students to a place where Eastern and Western cultures meet.
  • Several Turkish universities appear in the QS World University Rankings.

Is there a US–Türkiye E-2 treaty?

The basis for the E-2 visa

  • The treaty between the United States and Türkiye that underpins the E-2 visa dates from 1990 and has been in place ever since, with no fixed end date.
  • Any future change would depend on relations between the two countries and on what both governments agree.

Note: US policy and the conditions for granting an E-2 visa can still change, and the rules that apply are those in force when you file.

How well does the E-2 route work from Türkiye?

Türkiye and the E-2 route

  • Türkiye is one of the routes investors turn to most often when they are aiming at an E-2 visa through a second citizenship, because the investment level is reasonable, the requirements are simple, and processing is quick.
  • Türkiye holds a treaty with the United States covering both the E-1 and E-2 categories, and visas issued to Turkish nationals may run for up to 60 months — longer than for many other countries. The E-2 route suits investors with a genuine business plan in the United States.

Note: the E-2 is a nonimmigrant investor visa, not a green card or US citizenship.

Do I have to travel to Türkiye to obtain citizenship?

When you need to be in Türkiye

  • Under the current process you travel to Türkiye at least twice: once to file for your residence permit, and once to file your citizenship application and provide biometrics.
  • Everything else can be handled under power of attorney from your home country.
  • Once you hold citizenship, your family is not required to live in Türkiye.

Do I need to live in Türkiye to apply for an E-2 visa?

The condition attached to treaty-country citizenship

  • To apply for an E-2 visa, an investor who acquired Turkish citizenship through investment has to show a continuous 3-year connection with Türkiye under current US law. This is a legal test applied file by file, not simply a count of days spent in the country.
  • The exact conditions follow the E-2 rules in force when you file; check your position with an immigration attorney.

Up to what age can children be included?

Dependent children

  • The program allows unmarried children under 18 to be included in their parents' application.

Can parents be included?

Who counts as a dependent

  • Unfortunately not.
  • The program covers only you, your spouse, and dependent children under 18 in a single application.
  • If you need a route that covers parents as well, you could move to the Grenada citizenship program or a European program, which allow several generations in one application, or file a separate Turkish application for them.
