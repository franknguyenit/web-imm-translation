## Your route to Turkish citizenship

### Own qualifying real estate

If you own qualifying real estate, you can apply for Turkish citizenship.

### Family members included

The application can include your spouse and dependent children under 18, subject to the program rules.

### The route to the US E-2 visa

Once you hold Turkish citizenship, you can consider the US E-2 visa route if you meet the investment conditions and the separate US requirements.

The outcome depends on your case and on the decision of the competent authorities.

### Citizenship for life, and flexibility over your property

- Turkish citizenship is granted for life and does not have to be maintained through further investment; after 3 years you may sell the property and keep your citizenship.

### The opportunity to apply for a US E-2 visa

- Under US rules, an investor who obtained citizenship by investment must show at least 3 years of residence in Türkiye before applying for an E-2 visa. This is a US condition, not a Turkish one.
- The E-2 is a nonimmigrant visa; it is not a green card or US citizenship.
