[s005] Canada investor visa routes for entrepreneurs and investors — a path to Canadian permanent residence (PR).
[s009] What is Canada investment immigration?
[s010] A route for business owners, senior managers, and investors who want Canadian residence through business.
[s011] You establish, acquire, or operate a business in a Canadian province.
[s012] Once you meet the province's conditions, you can move toward Canadian permanent residence.
[s013] Each program suits a different financial capacity, management background, English level, and settlement plan.
[s016] Canada investment immigration{br}programs
[s017] The programs fall into 2 main groups: provincial entrepreneur streams, and the Start-Up Visa (SUV) for founders.
