## What is Canada investment immigration?

A route for business owners, senior managers, and investors who want Canadian residence through business.

You establish, acquire, or operate a business in a Canadian province.

Once you meet the province's conditions, you can move toward Canadian permanent residence.

Each program suits a different financial capacity, management background, English level, and settlement plan.

## Canada investment immigration programs

The programs fall into 2 main groups: provincial entrepreneur streams and the Start-Up Visa (SUV) for founders.

## Canadian immigration for founders (Start-Up Visa)

A program that grants permanent residence (PR) directly to entrepreneurs whose start-up idea is backed by a designated organization.

*Please note: the program stopped accepting new applications on January 1, 2026.
