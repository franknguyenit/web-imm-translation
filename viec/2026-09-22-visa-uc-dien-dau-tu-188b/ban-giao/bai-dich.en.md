# Australia 188B Investor Visa

### What is the 188B visa?

- The 188B Investor visa was the Investor stream of Australia's Business Innovation and Investment Program, for investors with a track record in business or investment and substantial financial capacity.
- Investors had to place at least A$2,500,000 in complying investments in Australia.
- The investment has to be held until the 888B permanent visa is granted, which cannot happen until at least 3 years later.
- A spouse or partner and children under 23 could be included in the same application.
- The program closed permanently to new applications on July 31, 2024. This page is a reference for applications already lodged and for the 888B stage that follows.

## Benefits of the 188B visa

### The whole family moves together

- A spouse or partner and children under 23 are granted visas alongside the main applicant.

### Living in Australia

- The whole family can live, study, work, and run a business in Australia while the visa is valid.

### Schooling in Australia

- Children can attend public school through Year 12 under the policy of each state or territory.

### Flexible travel

- Visa holders can travel freely between Australia and their home country while the visa is valid.

### A pathway to 888B permanent residence

- Holders can apply for the 888B permanent visa once the investment and residence requirements are met.

## Advantages of the 188B visa

### No business to run in Australia

- The capital is placed in complying investments, and day-to-day management of a business is not required.

### Returns from the investment

- Investors can receive distributions from the funds, depending on how those funds perform.

### A spouse can take on the main applicant role

- At the 888B stage, a spouse or partner included in the application can take over from the main applicant for certain requirements, where the rules allow.

### A clear pathway to permanent residence

- Once the investment has been held and the residence conditions met, the investor can apply for 888B permanent residence.

### Suited to financial investors

- Designed for investors with substantial financial capacity and a background in investment or business who did not want to run a business in Australia themselves.

## Who could apply for the 188B visa

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18–55.
- A track record in business or investment.
- Total net worth in business and personal assets of at least A$2,500,000.
- A score of at least 65 points on the points test.
- Nomination by an Australian state or territory.

### Experience requirements

- At least a 10% ownership stake in an operating company, with at least 3 years of direct management; or
- Evidence of managing an investment portfolio worth the equivalent of A$2,500,000 that produced a return in at least 3 fiscal years.

### Investment requirements in Australia

- Invest at least A$2,500,000 in complying investments as specified by the Australian Government.
- Hold that investment until the 888B permanent visa is granted.

### How the investment had to be allocated

- At least A$500,000 in venture capital and growth private equity funds.
- At least A$750,000 in managed funds investing in emerging companies listed on an Australian stock exchange.
- At least A$1,250,000 in a balancing investment.

## The 188B application process

### Assessment and case preparation

Review of the applicant's profile, assets, investment experience, and likely score on the points test.

### State or territory nomination

Preparing the application and applying for nomination from a suitable state or territory.

### Lodging the 188B application

Once nomination by a state or territory was granted, the 188B visa application was lodged.

### Making the investment when required

The capital is transferred into complying investments as required before the visa is granted.

### Holding the investment

The investment is held as required so that the conditions for the 888B permanent visa are met.

### Lodging the 888B application

Once the investment and residence conditions are met, the investor lodges the 888B permanent residence application.

## 188B visa FAQs

#### Is the 188B visa still open to new applications?

Program status

- No. The subclass 188 program closed permanently to new applications on July 31, 2024. Applications lodged before that date continue to be processed under the rules in force.

#### Did the 188B require running a business in Australia?

How the investment works

- No. The 188B was an investment stream based on complying investments, and it did not require the investor to run a business in Australia.

#### Was proof of source of funds required?

Source of funds

- Yes. Investors had to document a lawful and transparent source of funds and show how their assets had been accumulated.

#### Was English required?

English

- English was not a condition of lodging the application, but stronger English could add points and reduce the second instalment of the visa application charge payable by applicants without functional English.

#### How much residence was needed before the 888B application?

Residence

- The applicant has to meet the residence conditions set out in the rules before lodging the 888B permanent visa application.

#### Can a spouse take over from the main applicant at the 888B stage?

The spouse's role

- Yes, provided the spouse or partner holds the 188B visa as a family member and meets the conditions set out in the rules.

#### What are the main risks of the 188B?

Key risks

- The main risks lie in the proof of assets, source of funds, and investment experience, and in keeping the complying investment in place exactly as required.
