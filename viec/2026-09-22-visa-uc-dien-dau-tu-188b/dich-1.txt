[s003] What is the 188B visa?
[s004] The 188B Investor visa was the Investor stream of Australia's Business Innovation and Investment Program, for investors with a track record in business or investment and substantial financial capacity.
[s005] Investors had to place at least A$2,500,000 in complying investments in Australia.
[s006] The investment has to be held until the 888B permanent visa is granted, which can happen no earlier than 3 years later.
[s007] A spouse or partner and children under 23 could be included in the same application.
[s008|#bo-qua-so: ngày 31/07/2024 viết theo lối Mỹ "July 31, 2024" nên bản Anh không còn số 7 đứng riêng] The program closed permanently to new applications on July 31, 2024. This page is a reference for applications already lodged and for the 888B stage that follows.

[s010] Benefits of the 188B visa
[s011] The whole family moves together
[s012] A spouse or partner and children under 23 are granted visas alongside the main applicant.
[s013] Living in Australia
[s014] The whole family can live, study, work, and run a business in Australia while the visa is valid.
[s015] Schooling in Australia
[s016] Children can attend public school through Year 12 under the policy of each state or territory.
[s017] Flexible travel
[s018] Visa holders can travel freely between Australia and their home country while the visa is valid.
[s019] A pathway to 888B permanent residence
[s020] Holders can apply for the 888B permanent visa once the investment and residence requirements are met.
[s021] Advantages of the 188B visa
[s022] No business to run in Australia
[s023] The investment is made through complying funds, and day-to-day management of a business is not required.
[s024] Returns from the investment
[s025] Investors can receive distributions from the funds, depending on how those funds actually perform.
[s026] A spouse can take the lead role
[s027] At the 888B stage, a spouse or partner included in the application can step in for the main applicant to meet certain requirements, as the rules allow.
[s028] A clear pathway to permanent residence
[s029] Once the investment has been held and the residence conditions met, the investor can apply for 888B permanent residence.
[s030] Suited to financial investors
[s031] Designed for people with substantial financial capacity and a background in investment or business who did not want to run a business in Australia themselves.
[s032] Who could apply for the 188B visa
[s033] Applicant requirements
[s034] Aged 18-55.
[s035] A track record in business or investment.
[s036] Total net worth in business and personal assets of at least A$2,500,000.
[s037] A score of at least 65 points on the points test.
[s038] Nomination by an Australian state or territory.
[s039] Experience requirements
[s040] At least a 10% ownership stake in an operating company, with at least 3 years of direct management; or
[s041] Evidence of managing an investment portfolio worth the equivalent of A$2,500,000 that produced a return in at least 3 fiscal years.
[s042] Investment requirements in Australia
[s043] Invest at least A$2,500,000 in the complying investments set out by the Australian Government.
[s044] Hold that investment until the 888B permanent visa is granted.
[s045] How the investment had to be allocated
[s046] At least A$500,000 in venture capital and growth private equity funds.
[s047] At least A$750,000 in managed funds investing in emerging companies listed on an Australian exchange.
[s048] At least A$1,250,000 in a balancing investment.
[s049] The 188B application process
[s050] Assessment and case preparation
[s051] Review of the applicant's profile, assets, investment experience, and likely score on the points test.
[s052] State or territory nomination
[s053] Preparing the file and applying for nomination from a suitable state or territory.
[s054] Lodging the 188B application
[s055] Once nomination by a state or territory was granted, the 188B visa application was lodged.
[s056] Making the investment when required
[s057] The investor transfers the capital into complying funds as required before the visa is granted.
[s058] Holding the investment
[s059] The investment is held as required so that the conditions for the 888B permanent visa are met.
[s060] Lodging the 888B application
[s061] Once the investment and residence conditions are met, the investor lodges the 888B permanent residence application.

[s083] 188B visa FAQs
[s084] Is the 188B visa still open to new applications?
[s085] Program status
[s086|#bo-qua-so: ngày 31/07/2024 viết theo lối Mỹ "July 31, 2024" nên bản Anh không còn số 7 đứng riêng] No. The subclass 188 program closed permanently to new applications on July 31, 2024. Applications lodged before that date continue to be processed under the rules in force.
[s087] Did the 188B require running a business in Australia?
[s088] How the investment works
[s089] No. The 188B was an investment stream using complying funds, and it did not require the investor to run a business in Australia.
[s090] Was proof of source of funds required?
[s091] Source of funds
[s092] Yes. Investors had to document a lawful and transparent source of funds and show how their assets had been accumulated.
[s093] Was English required?
[s094] English
[s095] English was not a condition of lodging the application, but stronger English could add points and reduce the second visa application charge instalment payable for applicants without functional English.
[s096] How much residence was needed before the 888B application?
[s097] Residence
[s098] The applicant has to meet the residence conditions set out in the rules before lodging the 888B permanent visa application.
[s099] Can a spouse take over from the main applicant at the 888B stage?
[s100] The spouse's role
[s101] Yes, provided the spouse or partner held the 188B visa as a family member and meets the conditions set out in the rules.
[s102] What are the main risks of the 188B?
[s103] Key risks
[s104] The risks lie in evidencing assets, source of funds, and investment experience, and in keeping the complying investment in place exactly as required.
