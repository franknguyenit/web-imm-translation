# Cyprus Golden Visa

### What is the Cyprus Golden Visa?

- The Cyprus Golden Visa is a permanent residence program run by the Government of the Republic of Cyprus.
- It is open to non-EU investors and their families through an investment in Cypriot real estate from €300,000.
- No government donation is required, and there are no language, education, or management experience requirements.
- Cyprus is an EU member state and is working toward joining the Schengen Area.
- It can be a first step toward Cypriot citizenship once you meet the residence requirements.
- Who you can include: your legal spouse and children up to age 25 who are unmarried and financially dependent.
- Investment levels and rules can change over time.

## Cyprus permanent residence requirements

### Investment requirements

Invest at least €300,000, before VAT and other taxes and fees, in one of the following:

- Residential property: up to 2 new units bought from the developer, €300,000 or more in total; renting them out is permitted.
- Commercial property: offices, shops, hotels, and similar, €300,000 or more in total.
- Shares in a Cyprus company with genuine operations and at least 5 employees.
- Units in Cypriot investment funds — Alternative Investment Funds (AIF), Alternative Investment Funds with a Limited Number of Persons (AIFLNP), and Registered Alternative Investment Funds (RAIF).

### Main applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older, and able to pass the security and background checks.
- Secured annual income from outside Cyprus of at least €50,000, plus €15,000 for a spouse and €10,000 for each dependent child.
- Health insurance covering both inpatient and outpatient care.
- No employment in Cyprus, except as a director of the company if you choose the share investment route.

### Dependents

- Children under 18; or aged 18–25, unmarried, financially dependent, and in higher education.
- They must meet the security and background vetting requirements and hold health insurance.

## Cyprus Golden Visa FAQs

#### Which investments qualify?

Invest at least €300,000 in just one of the following:

- (1) up to 2 new residential units.
- (2) commercial property.
- (3) shares in a Cyprus company with at least 5 employees.
- (4) Cyprus investment funds (AIF/AIFLNP/RAIF).

Most investors choose an apartment for the long-term ownership and the option to rent it out.

#### What is Cypriot permanent residence, and how does it differ from citizenship?

Permanent residence is the right to live in Cyprus indefinitely — it is not citizenship.

- With permanent residence, your whole family can live in Cyprus, set up a business, and educate your children there.
- Once you meet the residence requirements, you may apply for Cypriot citizenship. Processing depends on your file and on the authorities.

#### How do Schengen and the EU differ?

Schengen is not the same as the EU

- The EU — the European Union: an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.
- Schengen: a border-free travel area of 29 countries.
- Some EU countries are not in Schengen, and the reverse is also true.
- Cyprus is an EU member state but is not part of the Schengen Area yet.

#### Does permanent residence give me free movement in Schengen and the EU?

You still need a visa for the EU and Schengen

- Because Cyprus is not in the Schengen Area and permanent residence does not grant free movement in the EU, you still need to apply for a visa — usually an eVisa, processed within a few days — when you travel to EU or Schengen countries.
- The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once you hold Cypriot citizenship.

#### Can I work in Cyprus on permanent residence?

You cannot take up employment

- Cypriot permanent residence does not allow you or your spouse to take up salaried employment.
- You may set up and run your own business. If you choose the share investment route, you can serve as a director of that company.

#### Can my children study free of charge?

Free of charge at Cypriot public schools

- From primary through the end of secondary school.

#### What are the requirements for Cypriot citizenship?

The main requirements

- Lawful residence in Cyprus for at least 8 years — 2,920 days — within an 11-year period.
- Continuous residence: you must have lived in Cyprus continuously for the 12 months immediately before you apply.
- Cumulative residence: in the 10 years before that final 12-month period, you must have lived in Cyprus for at least 7 years in total, or 2,555 days.
- You still hold the original investment, and you have no criminal record in Cyprus or abroad.
- Greek at CEFR level B1, and a basic knowledge of Cypriot society and institutions.

The requirements can change with the law — contact IMM Group for the current position.

#### What does Cypriot citizenship give you?

Full EU citizenship rights.

- The Cypriot passport gives visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to more than 170 countries and is ranked among the 15 strongest passports on current passport indexes; you can live, study, and work freely across the 27 EU member states, and dual citizenship is allowed.

#### What are the investment risks?

The 2 main risks

- Choosing a developer or project without a track record, or a property that does not qualify for permanent residence.
- Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.
- IMM Group reviews the project and your file in detail before any contract is signed, to limit these risks.

#### Is documenting my source of funds complicated?

Typically simpler than the United States, Australia, or Canada

- You need to document a lawful source of funds, at a basic level of detail.
- For funds from a property sale, for example, you will need to show the year of purchase and the value at the time.
- Every family documents this differently — contact IMM Group for advice on your own situation.

#### How long does permanent residence last, and how is it renewed?

Permanent residence has no expiry date as long as you keep the investment

- Cypriot permanent residence continues while you hold the investment and meet the conditions.
- Once you meet the residence requirements, you can apply for Cypriot citizenship.

#### Is there an age limit or an education requirement?

No upper age limit, and no education requirement

- The main applicant need only be 18 or older; no qualifications or management experience are required.

#### Which family members can be included?

Your spouse and dependent children

- Your legal spouse; and children, including adopted children, who are unmarried and financially dependent — under 18, or aged 18–25 and in higher education.
- Note: parents of the main applicant or of the spouse cannot be included in the same application.

#### Are adopted children and stepchildren eligible?

Adopted children and stepchildren are eligible

- Adopted child: a legal certificate is required.
- Stepchild: a legal certificate plus a notarized letter of consent from the spouse.
- IMM Group assists with preparing the documents.

#### How many days a year must I spend in Cyprus?

No minimum stay requirement

- You need only visit Cyprus once every 2 years to keep the card.
- If you are aiming for citizenship, you will need to meet the residence requirements set by Cypriot law.

#### When can I sell the property, shares, or fund units I invested in?

Keep the investment to keep permanent residence

- Once you hold Cypriot citizenship, you may transfer or sell the property, shares, or fund units you invested in.
