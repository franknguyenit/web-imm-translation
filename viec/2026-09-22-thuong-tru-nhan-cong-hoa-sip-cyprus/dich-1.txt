[s003] What is the Cyprus Golden Visa?
[s004] The Cyprus Golden Visa is a permanent residence program run by the Government of the Republic of Cyprus.
[s005] It is open to non-EU investors and their families through an investment in Cypriot real estate from €300,000.
[s006] No government donation, language, education, or management experience is required.
[s007] Cyprus is an EU member state and is working toward joining the Schengen Area.
[s008] It can be a first step toward Cypriot citizenship once you meet the residence requirements.
[s009] Who you can include: your legal spouse and children up to age 25 who are unmarried and financially dependent.
[s010] Investment levels and rules can change over time.

[s039] Cyprus permanent residence requirements
[s040] Investment requirements
[s041] Invest at least €300,000, before VAT and other taxes and fees, in one of the following:
[s042] Residential property: up to 2 new units bought from the developer, €300,000 or more in total; letting is permitted.
[s043] Commercial property: offices, shops, hotels, and similar, €300,000 or more in total.
[s044] Shares in a Cyprus company with genuine operations and at least 5 employees.
[s045] Units in Cyprus investment funds (AIF, AIFLNP, RAIF).
[s046] Main applicant requirements
[s047] Aged 18 or older, and meets the security and background vetting requirements.
[s048] Documented foreign income of at least €50,000 a year, plus €15,000 for a spouse and €10,000 for each dependent child.
[s049] Health insurance covering both inpatient and outpatient care.
[s050] No employment in Cyprus, except as a director of the company if you choose the share investment route.
[s051] Dependents
[s052] Children under 18; or aged 18–25, unmarried, financially dependent, and in higher education.
[s053] They must meet the security and background vetting requirements and hold health insurance.

[s088] Cyprus Golden Visa FAQs
[s089] Which investments qualify?
[s090] Invest at least €300,000 in ONE of the following:
[s091] (1) up to 2 new residential units.
[s092] (2) commercial property.
[s093] (3) shares in a Cyprus company with at least 5 employees.
[s094] (4) Cyprus investment funds (AIF/AIFLNP/RAIF).
[s095] Most investors choose an apartment, for the long-term ownership and the option to let it.
[s096] What is Cypriot permanent residence, and how does it differ from citizenship?
[s097] Permanent residence is the right to live in the country — it is not citizenship.
[s098] With permanent residence, the whole family can live in Cyprus, set up a business, and put the children through school there.
[s099] Once you meet the residence requirements, you can be assessed for Cypriot citizenship. Processing depends on your file and the authorities.
[s100] How do Schengen and the EU differ?
[s101] Schengen is not the same as the EU
[s102] The EU — the European Union: an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.
[s103] Schengen: a free-movement area of 29 countries.
[s104] Some EU countries are not in Schengen, and the reverse is also true.
[s105] Cyprus is an EU member state but is not part of the Schengen Area yet.
[s106] Does permanent residence give me free movement in Schengen and the EU?
[s107] You still need a visa for the EU and Schengen
[s108] Because Cyprus is not in the Schengen Area and permanent residence does not grant free movement in the EU, you still apply for a visa — usually an eVisa, processed within a few days — when you travel to EU or Schengen countries.
[s109] The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once you hold Cypriot citizenship.
[s110] Can I work in Cyprus on permanent residence?
[s111] You cannot take up employment
[s112] Cypriot permanent residence does not allow you or your spouse to work as an employee.
[s113] You may set up and run your own business. If you choose the share investment route, you can serve as a director of that company.
[s114] Can my children study free of charge?
[s115] Free of charge at Cypriot state schools
[s116] From primary through the end of secondary school.
[s117] What are the requirements for Cypriot citizenship?
[s118] The main requirements
[s119] Lawful residence in Cyprus for at least 8 years — 2,920 days — within an 11-year period.
[s120] Continuous residence: you must have lived in Cyprus continuously for the 12 months immediately before you apply.
[s121] Cumulative residence: in the 10 years before that final 12-month period, you must have lived in Cyprus for at least 7 years in total, or 2,555 days.
[s122] You keep the original investment, and have no criminal record in Cyprus or abroad.
[s123] Greek at level B1, and a basic knowledge of Cypriot society and institutions.
[s124] The requirements can change with the law — contact IMM Group for the current position.
[s125] What does Cypriot citizenship give you?
[s126] Full EU citizenship rights.
[s127] The Cypriot passport gives visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to more than 170 countries and ranks among the 15 strongest passports; you can live, study, and work freely across the 27 EU member states, and dual citizenship is allowed.

[s129] The 2 main risks
[s130] Choosing a developer or project without a track record, or a property that does not qualify for permanent residence.
[s131] Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to refusal.

[s134] Typically simpler than the United States, Australia, or Canada
[s135] You need to document a lawful source of funds, at a basic level of detail.
[s136] For funds from a property sale, for example, you will need to show the year of purchase and the value at the time.

[s138] How long does permanent residence last, and how is it renewed?
[s139] Permanent residence is held for life while you keep the investment
[s140] Cypriot permanent residence continues while you hold the investment and meet the conditions.
[s141] Once you meet the residence requirements, you can file for Cypriot citizenship.
[s142] Is there an age limit or an education requirement?
[s143] No upper age limit, and no education requirement
[s144] The main applicant need only be 18 or older; no qualifications or management experience are required.
[s145] Which family members can be included?
[s146] Your spouse and dependent children
[s147] Your legal spouse; and children, including adopted children, who are unmarried and financially dependent — under 18, or aged 18–25 and in higher education.
[s148] Note: the program does not allow the parents of the applicant or of the spouse to join the same application.
[s149] Are adopted children and stepchildren eligible?
[s150] Adopted children and stepchildren are eligible
[s151] Adopted child: a legal certificate is required.
[s152] Stepchild: a legal certificate plus a notarized letter of consent from the spouse.
[s153] IMM Group assists with preparing the documents.
[s154] How many days a year must I spend in Cyprus?
[s155] No minimum stay requirement
[s156] You need only visit Cyprus once every 2 years to keep the card.
[s157] If you are aiming at citizenship, you will need to meet the residence requirements set by Cypriot law.
[s158] When can I sell the property, shares, or fund units I invested in?
[s159] Keep the investment to keep permanent residence
[s160] Once you hold Cypriot citizenship, you may transfer or sell the property, shares, or fund units you invested in.
