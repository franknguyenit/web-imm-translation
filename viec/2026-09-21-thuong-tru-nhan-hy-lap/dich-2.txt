[s091] Frequently asked questions
[s092] What is the Greece Golden Visa, and how does it differ from permanent residence?
[s093] The Golden Visa is Greece's long-term residence permit for investors.
[s094] It carries rights equivalent to permanent residence: the whole family can live in Greece, open a business, and send their children to school there (employment is not permitted).
[s095] The permit is valid for 5 years and is renewed every 5 years as long as the conditions are still met.
[s096] It can serve as a stepping stone toward Greek citizenship if the investor meets the residence, language, and integration conditions set by Greek law.
[s097] Does the Greece Golden Visa allow free movement across the Schengen Area and the EU?
[s098] Short-term travel within the Schengen Area — not the right to live or work across the EU
[s099] The Golden Visa allows travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rule of 90 days in any six-month period.
[s100] Travel to EU countries outside the Schengen Area still requires a visitor visa under each country's own rules.
[s101] The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once the investor holds Greek citizenship.
[s102] What is the difference between the Schengen Area and the EU?
[s103] Schengen ≠ EU
[s104] The EU — the European Union: an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.
[s105] The Schengen Area: a zone that allows free movement between its member countries.
[s106] Some EU countries are outside the Schengen Area, and some Schengen countries are outside the EU.
[s107] Greece is a member of both the Schengen Area and the EU.
