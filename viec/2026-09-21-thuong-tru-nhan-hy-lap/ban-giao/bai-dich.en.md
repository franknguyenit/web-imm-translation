# Greece Golden Visa (Greek Residency by Investment)

### What is the Greece Golden Visa?

- The Greece Golden Visa is a residence permit program established by the Greek government.
- Open to non-EU investors and their families who invest in Greek real estate.
- Three generations of your family can live in Greece and travel across the 29 countries of the Schengen Area.
- Minimum investment of €250,000.
- The €250,000 threshold applies to the change of use category, where commercial property is converted to residential use; taxes and fees are not included.

## Greece Golden Visa requirements

### Real estate investment

- €250,000 — commercial property converted to residential use, or a listed heritage building requiring restoration; available in any location, including central Athens
- €400,000 — real estate in all other areas, with a minimum of 120 sq m
- €800,000 — real estate in Attica (Athens), Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini and islands with more than 3,100 residents, with a minimum of 120 sq m

- Meet the security and health requirements

- No salaried employment in Greece

- Your legal spouse

## Frequently asked questions

#### What is the Greece Golden Visa, and how does it differ from permanent residence?

- The Golden Visa is Greece's long-term residence permit for investors.
- It carries much the same day-to-day rights as permanent residence: you and your family can live in Greece, open a business, and send your children to school there (salaried employment is not permitted).
- The permit is valid for 5 years and is renewable in 5-year terms as long as you continue to meet the conditions.
- It can also be a first step toward Greek citizenship, provided you meet the residence, language, and integration conditions set by Greek law.

#### Does the Greece Golden Visa allow free movement across the Schengen Area and the EU?

- Short-term travel within the Schengen Area — not the right to live or work across the EU
- The Golden Visa allows travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rule of 90 days in any 180-day period.
- Travel to EU countries outside the Schengen Area still requires a visitor visa under each country's own rules.
- The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once you hold Greek citizenship.

#### What is the difference between the Schengen Area and the EU?

The Schengen Area is not the same thing as the EU

- The European Union (EU): an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.
- The Schengen Area: a zone that allows free movement between its member countries.
- Some EU countries are outside the Schengen Area, and some Schengen countries are outside the EU.
- Greece is a member of both the Schengen Area and the EU.

#### Can my children study in Greece for free?

- Children study free of charge at Greek public schools.
- From primary school through postgraduate study.

#### What are the risks of a Greece Golden Visa investment?

A Golden Visa investment carries 2 main risks.

- Choosing a project or developer without a solid track record, or a project that does not qualify.
- Investment funds that are not lawfully sourced, which leads to the application being denied.

#### How long is the Greece Golden Visa valid, and how is it renewed?

- A 5-year permit, renewable every 5 years

Renewal conditions

- You still own the real estate
- A clean criminal record
- After 7 years: you may apply for Greek citizenship if you meet the residence and language conditions.

#### How do dependents renew their residence permits?

- Main applicant and spouse: renewal works the same way, with no limit on the number of renewals, as long as you still own the real estate and your criminal record remains clean.
- Dependent children: renewal is possible only up to age 24

Three options once a child turns 24:

- The child applies for Greek citizenship, if eligible, to keep the right of residence.
- Under the new rules, a child under 18 is named as the main applicant.
- The real estate is transferred to the child, who can then keep renewing — though this route involves more time and cost.

#### How many days a year do I have to spend in Greece?

There is no continuous residence requirement to maintain the Golden Visa.

- You do not need to live in Greece continuously to hold and renew the Golden Visa.
- If you plan to apply for Greek citizenship later, you will need to meet the residence and integration conditions set by Greek law.

#### What are the requirements to move from the Greece Golden Visa to citizenship?

Five conditions for Greek citizenship:

- 7 years of physical residence in Greece — at least 183 days a year
- A source of income in Greece
- Greek language ability at B1 level, plus a pass in the citizenship exam on history, culture, and institutions
- Proof of integration into Greek society
- No criminal record in Greece or abroad

Separate conditions for children:

- Nine years of schooling in Greece (primary and secondary), or
- Six years of schooling in Greece (secondary) before the age of 18

- Full EU citizenship rights.
