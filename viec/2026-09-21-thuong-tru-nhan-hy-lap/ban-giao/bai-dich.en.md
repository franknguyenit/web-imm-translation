# Greece Golden Visa (Residency by Investment)

### What is the Greece Golden Visa?

- The Greece Golden Visa is a residence permit program established by the Greek government.
- It is open to non-EU investors and their families through an investment in Greek real estate.
- Three generations of your family can live in Greece and travel across the 29 Schengen countries.
- Minimum investment: €250,000.
- The €250,000 threshold applies to properties converted from commercial to residential use; taxes and fees are not included.

## Greece Golden Visa investment requirements

### Real estate investment

- €250,000 — commercial property converted to residential use, or a historic building in need of restoration; available in any location, including central Athens
- €400,000 — property in all other areas, minimum 120 m²
- €800,000 — property in Attica (Athens), Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini, and islands with more than 3,100 residents, minimum 120 m²

### Applicant requirements

- You meet the background security and health requirements
- You document a lawful source of funds
- You do not take up salaried employment in Greece

You may make further investments or set up a business.

### Family members who can be included

- Your legal spouse
- Children (including adopted children) up to age 21; a child's card can be renewed until age 24
- Both sets of parents — yours and your spouse's

## Frequently asked questions

#### What is a Golden Visa, and is it different from permanent residence?

- The Golden Visa is Greece's long-term residence permit for investors.
- In practice it gives your family many of the same day-to-day rights as permanent residence: you can live in Greece, start a business, and send your children to school (salaried employment is not permitted).
- The card is valid for 5 years and is renewable every 5 years, provided the conditions continue to be met.
- It can put you on the pathway to Greek citizenship if you meet the residence, language, and integration conditions set by law.

#### Does the Golden Visa allow free movement in the Schengen Area and the EU?

- Short stays in the Schengen Area — not a right to live or work elsewhere in the EU
- The Golden Visa allows travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules of 90 days in any 180-day period.
- Travel to EU countries outside the Schengen Area still requires a visitor visa under each country's rules.
- The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once you hold Greek citizenship.

#### What is the difference between Schengen and the EU?

Schengen is not the same as the EU

- The EU — the European Union: an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.
- Schengen: an area of free movement between its member countries.
- Some EU countries are not in Schengen, and the reverse is also true.
- Greece is a member of both the Schengen Area and the EU.

#### Can my children study free of charge?

- Free of charge at Greek public institutions.
- From primary school through postgraduate study.

There are 2 main risks in a Golden Visa investment.

- Choosing a project or developer without a solid track record, or a project that does not qualify.
- Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to refusal.

#### How long is the Golden Visa valid, and how is it renewed?

- A 5-year card, renewable every 5 years

Renewal conditions

- You still own the property
- A clean criminal record (police clearance certificate)
- After 7 years: you may apply for Greek citizenship if you meet the residence and language conditions.

#### How are dependents' cards renewed?

- Main applicant and spouse: renewals on the same terms, with no limit on the number of renewals, as long as you still own the property and your criminal records remain clean.
- Dependent children: renewals are available only until age 24

There are 3 options once a child turns 24:

- Your child applies for Greek citizenship, if eligible, to keep the right of residence.
- You name a child under 18 as the main applicant under the new rules.
- You transfer the property to your child so that they can keep renewing, though this takes more time and costs more.

#### How many days a year must I spend in Greece?

No minimum stay is required to keep the Golden Visa.

- You do not need to live in Greece to hold and renew your Golden Visa.
- If you plan to apply for Greek citizenship later, you will need to meet the residence and integration conditions set by Greek law.

#### What are the requirements for Greek citizenship?

The 5 conditions for Greek naturalization:

- Actual residence in Greece for 7 full years — at least 183 days each year
- A source of income in Greece
- Greek language at B1 level, plus passing the citizenship exam on history, culture, and institutions
- Evidence of integration into Greek society
- No criminal record in Greece or abroad

Separate route for children:

- Schooling in Greece for 9 years (primary and secondary), or
- Schooling in Greece for 6 years (secondary) before age 18

#### What does Greek citizenship give you?

- Full EU citizenship rights.
- Freedom to live, work, and study in any EU country.
