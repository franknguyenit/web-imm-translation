[s003] What is the Greece Golden Visa?
[s004] A residence permit program established by the Greek government.
[s005] Open to non-EU investors and their families through real estate investment.
[s006] Three generations of one family can live in Greece and travel across 29 Schengen Area countries.
[s007|#bo-qua-so: số VND chỉ nhắc lại mức 250.000 EUR, khách quốc tế không cần] From €250,000 as the minimum investment.
[s008] The €250,000 threshold applies to the change-of-use route, where commercial property is converted to residential; taxes and fees are not included.

[s010] Greece Golden Visa benefits
[s011] Live in Greece
[s012] Live, invest, run a business, and study freely in Greece.
[s013] Freedom of travel across the Schengen Area
[s014] Travel to 29 Schengen Area countries under the short-stay rules of 90 days in any six-month period.
[s015] Access to education on the same terms as Greek nationals
[s016] Your children study free of charge at Greek public schools, from primary school through public university.
[s017] From the Greece Golden Visa to Greek citizenship
[s018] After a period of lawful residence, and once the residence, language and integration conditions set by Greek law are met, investors may become eligible to apply for citizenship.
[s019] Once you hold Greek citizenship
[s020] Visa-free or eVisa access to more than 180 destinations, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, and countries across Europe.
[s021] Greece Golden Visa advantages
[s022] Greece Golden Visa cost from €250,000
[s023] Own real estate in central Athens from €250,000 — about one third of the €800,000 threshold that otherwise applies in the area — thanks to the change-of-use route for property converted to residential.

[s032] No education requirement

[s039] Greece Golden Visa requirements
[s040] Real estate investment
[s041] €250,000 — commercial property converted to residential use, or a listed heritage building requiring restoration; available in any location, including central Athens
[s042] €400,000 — real estate in all other areas, with a minimum of 120 sq m
[s043] €800,000 — real estate in Attica (Athens), Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini and islands with more than 3,100 residents, with a minimum of 120 sq m
[s044] Applicant requirements
[s045] Meet security and medical background requirements
[s046] Provide proof of a lawful source of funds
[s047] No salaried employment in Greece
[s048] Further investment and setting up a company are permitted.
[s049] Family members you can include
[s050] Your legal spouse
[s051] Children, including adopted children, up to age 21; a child's permit can be renewed until age 24
[s052] The parents of both spouses
