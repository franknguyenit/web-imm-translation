[s092] What is a Golden Visa, and is it different from permanent residence?
[s093] The Golden Visa is Greece's long-term residence permit for investors.
[s094] It carries rights equivalent to permanent residence: your whole family can live in Greece, start a business, and send your children to school (salaried employment is not permitted).
[s095] The card is valid for 5 years and is renewed every 5 years when the conditions are met.
[s096] It can be a stepping stone toward Greek citizenship if you meet the residence, language, and integration conditions set by law.
[s097] Does the Golden Visa allow free movement in the Schengen Area and the EU?
[s098] Short stays in the Schengen Area — not yet the right to live or work across the EU
[s099|#bo-qua-so: "6 tháng" viết đúng luật Schengen là "any 180-day period"; số 90 ngày giữ nguyên] The Golden Visa allows travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules of 90 days in any 180-day period.
[s100] Travel to EU countries outside the Schengen Area still requires a visitor visa under each country's rules.
[s101] The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once you hold Greek citizenship.
[s102] What is the difference between Schengen and the EU?
[s103] Schengen ≠ EU
[s104] The EU — the European Union: an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.
[s105] Schengen: an area of free movement between its member countries.
[s106] Some EU countries are not in Schengen, and the reverse is also true.
[s107] Greece is a member of BOTH the Schengen Area and the EU.
