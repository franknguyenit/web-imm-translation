[s003] What is the New Brunswick Business Immigration Stream?
[s004] A Canadian permanent residence pathway for business owners and senior managers who want to invest in, start, or acquire a business in New Brunswick.
[s005] You must run the business in the province yourself in order to qualify for a provincial nomination and move toward Canadian permanent residence.
[s006] Your spouse and your children under 22 can be included in the same application.

[s008] New Brunswick is well placed for trade with the United States and Quebec, has a reasonable cost of living, and is home to several major universities and colleges.
[s009] A recent update: the province now calls this the Business Immigration Stream, replacing the former Entrepreneurial Stream. Under the current rules, you can apply for a nomination once your business has operated for 6 continuous months, provided you meet the program conditions.

[s086] Frequently asked questions about New Brunswick
[s087] Is a work permit the same as permanent residence?
[s088] Is a work permit the same as permanent residence
[s089] No. A work permit gives you the temporary right to live and work in Canada. You need to complete your investment commitment, operate the business, and meet the residence conditions before you can apply for permanent residence.
[s090] Do you have to run the business yourself?
[s091] Do you have to run the business yourself
[s092] Yes. You must manage and run the business in New Brunswick in person. IMM Group and our partners can support you with the business plan, but we cannot operate the business on your behalf.
[s093] Is an exploratory visit to New Brunswick mandatory?
[s094] Is an exploratory visit to New Brunswick mandatory
[s095] An exploratory visit is not required before you apply. That said, visiting in person can help you understand the local market and prepare a stronger business plan.
[s096] When can you apply for the provincial nomination?
[s097] When can you apply for the provincial nomination
[s098] You can apply for a nomination once you have operated your business in New Brunswick for at least 6 continuous months and met the program conditions.
[s099] Does your family have to live in New Brunswick?
[s100] Does your family have to live in New Brunswick
[s101] Before you hold permanent residence, you and your family are expected to settle in the province you applied to and to honor your residence and business commitments.
[s102] Can you live in another province once you hold permanent residence?
[s103] Can you live in another province once you hold permanent residence
[s104] Once you hold Canadian permanent residence, your family has the right to live and work in any province, as permanent residence allows.
