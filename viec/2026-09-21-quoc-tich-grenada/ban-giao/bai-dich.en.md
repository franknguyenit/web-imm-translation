#### Do I need to have lived in Grenada to apply for an E-2 visa?

The E-2 visa requires 3 years of domicile in Grenada

- Grenadian citizens who acquired citizenship through investment must be domiciled in Grenada for 3 continuous years before filing an E-2 application — a US requirement that applies to citizenship obtained through an investment program. Domicile means actual residence with the intention of remaining, which is a stricter test than holding the passport.
- It is the only route to the E-2 visa in the Caribbean: Grenada is the only Caribbean country with an E-2 treaty with the US, in force since 1989.
