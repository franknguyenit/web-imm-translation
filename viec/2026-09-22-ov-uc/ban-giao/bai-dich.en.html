Overview

Overview

What is the Australia investment visa?

  • A route for business owners and investors who want Australian residence through a business or an investment.
  • Your family can be included on the application and can live, study and work in Australia for the life of the visa.
  • The usual route: the temporary 188 visa, then permanent residence on the 888 visa, then Australian citizenship once you meet the conditions.
  • Each stream suits a different level of capital, management experience and migration goal.

What the program is for

  • A temporary Australian visa, valid for 5 years
  • Permanent residence once you meet the business conditions in Australia
  • A path to Australian citizenship

How you invest

Start a new business, or buy an existing one, and run it yourself in Australia.

Applicant requirements

  • Under 55, with a score of at least 65 on Australia's migration points test
  • A business owner or a shareholder involved in management, with a successful business in your home country
  • Total assets of at least A$1.25 million
  • Nomination by an Australian state or territory

Company requirements

  • At least 30% of the business, with at least 3 years running it directly
  • In 2 of the past 4 financial years, company turnover of at least A$750,000 a year

The route to permanent residence on the 888A

  • Run the Australian business and hold at least 30% of it; invest from A$300,000, depending on the state
  • The business trading for at least 2 years, with turnover of A$300,000 in at least 1 of them
  • Meet 2 of these 3: net business assets of at least A$300,000 · combined personal and business assets of at least A$600,000 · at least 2 full-time jobs created
  • At least 1 year of residence in the 3 years before you apply
  • Either the main applicant or the spouse on the 188A visa can be the applicant

Key benefits

The whole family — spouse and children under 23 — holds temporary residence and can live, work and study in Australia

Free public schooling for your children through high school

Once you qualify, you apply for permanent residence on the 888A, with access to healthcare, education and social security, except the right to vote

After about 4 years of residence, including 1 year as a permanent resident, you can be considered for citizenship, with visa-free access to 187 countries

Please note

You have to run the Australian business yourself

It requires genuine time living in Australia and running the business

It suits business owners with genuine management experience

What the program is for

  • A temporary Australian visa, valid for 5 years
  • Permanent residence after a minimum of 3 years holding the investment
  • A path to Australian citizenship

Applicant requirements

  • Under 55, with a score of at least 65 on Australia's migration points test
  • Experience in business or investing, and substantial financial capacity
  • Total assets of at least A$2.5 million
  • Nomination by an Australian state or territory

Business or investment experience

  • Hold at least 10% of an operating business and run it for at least 3 years, OR
  • Show that you have managed an investment of about A$2.5 million that produced a return over at least 3 years

Investment structure — a minimum of A$2.5 million

  • At least A$500,000 — venture capital and private equity funds
  • At least A$750,000 — funds holding emerging and growth companies listed in Australia
  • At least A$1.25 million — balanced investment funds
  • Hold the investment for at least 3 years, until the 888B is granted

The route to permanent residence on the 888B

  • Keep the investment in place until the 888B is granted
  • At least 2 years of residence in total before you apply
  • Either the main applicant or the spouse can be the applicant

Key benefits

The whole family holds temporary residence and can live, work and study in Australia

Free public schooling for your children through high school

You receive the returns the fund actually generates

Once you qualify, you apply for permanent residence on the 888B, and can be considered for citizenship after about 4 years of residence

Please note

The investment has to be held for a long period

At least 2 years of residence in total are needed before you file the 888B

It suits investors with substantial capital who do not want to run a business themselves

The route

The route

From temporary residence to Australian citizenship

Four clear milestones for your family — from the 188 visa to Australian citizenship.

1

Visa 188A / 188B / 188C

Temporary residence

  • The whole family — the applicant, spouse and dependent children under 23 — moves to Australia to live, work and study for the 5-year life of the visa
  • Free public schooling for your children through high school

2

Hold the 188 visa for at least 3 years

Carry out the investment or run the business

  • 188A: run the Australian business continuously for at least 2 years; turnover of at least A$300,000 over the most recent 12 months; at least 1 year of residence in the 3 years before you file the 888A
  • 188B: keep the A$2.5 million investment in place until the 888B is granted; at least 2 years of residence in total
  • 188C: keep the A$5 million investment in place; the main applicant spends at least 40 days a year in Australia, OR the accompanying spouse at least 180 days a year

3

Visa 888A / 888B / 888C

Permanent residence

  • Meet the investment or business conditions and the residence conditions, and the whole family applies for permanent residence — at the earliest after 3 years on the 188 visa
  • Full access to Medicare, education and social security on the same basis as Australian citizens, except the right to vote
  • The permanent residence card is renewed every 5 years

4

Australian citizenship

Australian citizenship

  • At least 4 years of lawful residence, with the most recent 12 months as a permanent resident on the 888 visa
  • No more than 12 months outside Australia across those 4 years, and no more than 90 days away in the final 12 months
  • With citizenship: full civic rights, plus visa-free access to 187 countries
