[s017] What is the Australia investment visa?
[s018] A route for business owners and investors who want Australian residence through a business or an investment.
[s019] Your family can be included on the application and can live, study and work in Australia for the life of the visa.
[s020] The usual route: the temporary 188 visa, then permanent residence on the 888 visa, then Australian citizenship once you meet the conditions.
[s021] Each stream suits a different level of capital, management experience and migration goal.

[s213|#bo-qua-so: so 4 dau cau viet bang chu "Four"] Four clear milestones for your family — from the 188 visa to Australian citizenship.
[s214] 1
[s215] Visa 188A / 188B / 188C
[s216] Temporary residence
[s217] The whole family — the applicant, spouse and dependent children under 23 — moves to Australia to live, work and study for the life of the visa, which is 5 years
[s218] Free public schooling for your children through high school
[s219] 2
[s220] Hold the 188 visa for at least 3 years
[s221] Carry out the investment or run the business
[s222] **188A:** run the Australian business for at least 2 years without a break; turnover of at least A$300,000 over the most recent 12 months; at least 1 year of residence in the 3 years before you file the 888A
[s223] **188B:** keep the A$2.5 million investment in place until the 888B is granted; at least 2 years of residence in total
[s224] **188C:** keep the A$5 million investment in place; the main applicant spends at least 40 days a year in Australia, OR the accompanying spouse at least 180 days a year
[s225] 3
[s226] Visa 888A / 888B / 888C
[s227] Permanent residence
[s228] Meet the investment or business conditions and the residence conditions, and the whole family applies for permanent residence — at the earliest after 3 years on the 188 visa
[s229] Full access to Medicare, education and social security on the same basis as Australian citizens, voting aside
[s230] The permanent residence card is renewed every 5 years
[s231] 4
[s232] Australian citizenship
[s233] Australian citizenship
[s234] At least 4 years of lawful residence, with the most recent 12 months as a permanent resident on the 888 visa
[s235] No more than 12 months outside Australia across those 4 years, and no more than 90 days away in the final 12 months
[s236] With citizenship: full civic rights, plus visa-free access to 187 countries
