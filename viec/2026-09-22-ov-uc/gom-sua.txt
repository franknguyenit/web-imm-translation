[s006] The Australia investment visa: business and investor routes to residence for your whole family
[s011] Australia's Business Innovation and Investment (subclass 188) visas are not accepting new applications. IMM Group advises on the route that fits your family, based on where each program stands and on your own circumstances.
[s013] What follows gives you an overview of the 188A, 188B and 188C streams, so you can compare them if the program reopens, or weigh the alternatives.
[s071] Residence needed
[s074] Main applicant 40+ days/yr; spouse 180+ days/yr
[s078] {1}A{/1} Investment visa · 188A
[s102] At least 30% of the business, with at least 3 years running it directly
[s113] Once you qualify, you apply for permanent residence on the 888A, with access to healthcare, education and social security, except the right to vote
[s117] It requires genuine time living in Australia and running the business
[s123] {1}B{/1} Investment visa · 188B
[s147] Investment structure — a minimum of A$2.5 million
[s150] At least A$1.25 million — balanced investment funds
[s169] {1}C{/1} Investment visa · 188C
[s177] a year (average, not guaranteed)
[s188] Nomination by an Australian state or territory
[s190] Investment structure — A$5 million
[s193] At least A$2.5 million — balanced investment funds
[s205] A high capital requirement
[s217] The whole family — the applicant, spouse and dependent children under 23 — moves to Australia to live, work and study for the 5-year life of the visa
[s222] **188A:** run the Australian business continuously for at least 2 years; turnover of at least A$300,000 over the most recent 12 months; at least 1 year of residence in the 3 years before you file the 888A
[s229] Full access to Medicare, education and social security on the same basis as Australian citizens, except the right to vote
