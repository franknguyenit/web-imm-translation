[s003] What is the Manitoba Entrepreneur Pathway?
[s004] The pathway is designed for business owners and senior managers who want to invest in, acquire or operate a business in Manitoba.
[s005] You must operate the business in the province yourself to qualify for Canadian permanent residence.
[s006] Your spouse and children under 22 can be included in the same application.
[s007] The investment starts at C$150,000 for a business outside the Manitoba Capital Region, or C$250,000 for a business inside it.

[s009] Benefits of immigrating to Manitoba
[s010] Your whole family moves to Manitoba
[s011] You are free to live, study and work in Manitoba while you hold your work permit.
[s012] Your children study in Canada
[s013] Children attend public school free of charge through the end of high school; once your family holds permanent residence, they can attend university at domestic tuition rates.
[s014] A path to permanent residence
[s015] Once you have met your commitments to the province, you and your family can apply for Canadian permanent residence.
[s016] A route to Canadian citizenship
[s017] After obtaining permanent residence and meeting the residency, tax, language and background requirements, your family can consider applying for Canadian citizenship.
[s018] Why investors choose this program
[s019] No age limit
[s020] The program sets no maximum age, although applicants aged 25–49 are given priority.
[s021] No deposit required
[s022] The province does not require you to place a deposit to take part in the program.
[s023] An investment starting at C$150,000
[s024] This level applies outside the Manitoba Capital Region; inside the capital region the minimum investment is C$250,000.
[s025] Only one job to create or maintain
[s026] Your Manitoba business must create or maintain at least one job for a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.
[s027] Applicant requirements
[s028] You are a business owner or senior manager
[s029] At least 3 years of business management experience within the previous 5 years.
[s030] Completion of high school.
[s031] English at a level equivalent to IELTS General Training 5.0.
[s032] A minimum net worth of C$500,000.
[s033] Clear security, background and medical checks.
[s034] Investment requirements
[s035] A minimum investment of C$250,000 for a business in the Manitoba Capital Region.
[s036] A minimum investment of C$150,000 for a business outside the capital region.
[s037] A viable business plan that delivers economic benefit to the province.
[s038] Hands-on operation of the business, creating or maintaining at least one job for a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.
[s039] The Manitoba application process
[s040] Assessment and case preparation
[s041] We assess your eligibility, your assets, your management experience, your English-language ability and your overall fit with the program.
[s042] Exploratory visit to Manitoba
[s043] You travel to Manitoba to assess the business environment and prepare your investment plan.
[s044] Submit your Expression of Interest (EOI)
[s045] You submit your Expression of Interest to the Government of Manitoba.
[s046] Receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) and submit your application
[s047] Once you are invited, you complete the formal application for the province.
[s048] Provincial interview
[s049] You attend an interview with the Government of Manitoba.
[s050] Work permit application
[s051] Once the province approves your application, you apply to the federal government for a work permit.
[s052] Move to Manitoba to invest and run your business
[s053] With your work permit issued, you move to Manitoba, make the investment and run the business in person.
[s054] Apply for permanent residence
[s055] After meeting the business and residency conditions, you apply for Canadian permanent residence.

[s078] Frequently asked questions about Manitoba
[s079] Is a work permit the same as permanent residence?
[s080] Is a work permit the same as permanent residence
[s081] No. A work permit gives you temporary permission to live and work in Canada. You must complete your investment, business operating and residency commitments before you can apply for permanent residence.
[s082] Do I have to run the business myself?
[s083] Do I have to run the business myself
[s084] Yes. You must personally manage and operate the business in Manitoba. IMM Group and our partners can help with the business plan, but we cannot run the business on your behalf.
[s085] Does my family have to live in Manitoba?
[s086] Does my family have to live in Manitoba
[s087] Before you hold permanent residence, you need to stay closely involved with the business in the province you applied to. Your family must live in line with the commitments you made under the program.
[s088] When can I move up to permanent residence?
[s089] When can I move up to permanent residence
[s090] Once you have operated the business, met the residency conditions and completed your commitments to the province, you can apply for permanent residence.
[s091] Do I have to prove the source of my funds?
[s092] Do I have to prove the source of my funds
[s093] Yes. You must show that your funds and assets are lawful and clearly documented. Eligible assets can include real estate, business assets, savings, and shares or stock.
[s094] After permanent residence, can the family live in another province?
[s095] After permanent residence, can the family live in another province
[s096] Once you hold Canadian permanent residence, you and your family are free to live and work in any province.
[s097] What are the main risks of the program?
[s098] What are the main risks of the program
[s099] Risk usually comes from a business plan that is not viable, failing to operate the business as committed, not creating or maintaining the required job, or weak documentation of your finances.
