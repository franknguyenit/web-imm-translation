# New Zealand Business Investor Visa

### What is the Business Investor Visa?

- The New Zealand Business Investor Visa is for investors who want to settle in New Zealand by investing in an operating business.
- The minimum investment is NZ$1 million.
- In the first stage, you and your family are granted a visa valid for up to 4 years so that you can manage and grow the business yourself.
- There are 2 investment options: from NZ$1 million, with a Resident Visa application after 3 years; or from NZ$2 million on the fast route, with a Resident Visa application after 12 months.
- Once you hold the Resident Visa and have lived in New Zealand for the required time, you can consider the route to citizenship.

## Business Investor Visa benefits

### The whole family moves to New Zealand

- You, your spouse, and your dependent children can live, study, and work in New Zealand, subject to the conditions of the visa.

### Access to the public health system

- Your family can access New Zealand's public health system, with the entitlements and conditions that apply to each visa or residence status.

### Your children study in New Zealand

- Your children can attend public schools through the end of secondary school, with the entitlements that match your family's visa or residence status.

### A route to the Resident Visa

- You can be considered for a New Zealand Resident Visa after at least 12 months or 3 years of running the business, depending on your investment route and whether you meet the conditions in full.

### The route to New Zealand citizenship

- Once you hold the Resident Visa and meet the physical residence conditions, you can consider applying for New Zealand citizenship.
- With citizenship, your family holds New Zealand passports and travels internationally more easily; the number of countries offering visa-free or eVisa entry changes over time.

## Advantages of the Business Investor Visa

### A fast route to the Resident Visa

- On the route from NZ$2 million, you can be considered for a Resident Visa after 12 months of running the business, provided you meet the program conditions.

### A more accessible entry level than the Active Investor Plus Visa

- The investment starts at NZ$1 million, below the threshold for the Active Investor Plus Visa.

### Direct control over your investment

- You take part directly in managing and growing the business in New Zealand, which gives you more control over operating strategy and how the capital is used.

## Business Investor Visa requirements

### Applicant requirements

- Under 55.
- English at IELTS 5.0 or an equivalent certificate.
- Relevant experience managing and running a business.
- Meets the health and police clearance certificate requirements.
- You document a lawful source of funds for the investment.
- At least a further NZ$500,000 in settlement funds for your family's first period in New Zealand.

### Requirements for the business you invest in

- You invest in a business already operating in New Zealand.
- You can buy the business outright or hold at least 25% of its shares.
- The business must meet the program's requirements on trading history, legal standing, finances, and ability to operate.
- The business must maintain at least 5 full-time jobs.
- It must create at least 1 new job for a New Zealand citizen or permanent resident.

### Investment routes

- Standard route: invest at least NZ$1 million.
- After running the business and meeting the conditions for 3 years, you can apply for a Resident Visa.
- Fast route: invest at least NZ$2 million.
- After running the business and meeting the conditions for 12 months, you can apply for a Resident Visa.

### Operating and residence conditions

- You must manage and grow the business in New Zealand yourself.
- At least 184 days a year in New Zealand.
- You keep the business running in line with the plan that was approved.
- You meet the requirements on maintaining jobs, creating new jobs, complying with the law, and meeting your financial and tax obligations in New Zealand.

## The Business Investor Visa process

### Find and assess a business

We look for an operating New Zealand business that is a suitable investment. We assess its finances, its legal standing, and its operating potential in detail.

### Prepare the file

We gather your personal documents, evidence of your experience, and your English certificate. We prepare the evidence of your source of funds and your settlement funds, together with the business plan.

### File the Business Investor Work Visa application

The complete application is submitted to Immigration New Zealand for a Business Investor Work Visa, valid for up to 4 years.

### Approval and completion of the investment

Once your application is approved, you follow the next steps as instructed to complete the investment and have the visa issued.

### Move to New Zealand and run the business

You arrive in New Zealand and begin running the business yourself.

### Apply for the Resident Visa

After 12 months on the fast route, or 3 years on the standard route, you can apply for a Business Investor Resident Visa, provided you meet all the conditions.

### A route to citizenship

Once you have held residence and physically lived in New Zealand for the required time, you can consider applying for New Zealand citizenship.

## Business Investor Visa FAQs

#### Is the Business Investor Visa a Resident Visa from the start?

- No. The first stage is a Business Investor Work Visa, valid for up to 4 years. You need to make the investment, move to New Zealand, and run the business yourself before you qualify to be considered for a Resident Visa.

#### What is the minimum investment?

- The minimum investment is NZ$1 million on the standard route. To be considered for a Resident Visa on the fast route after 12 months, you need to invest at least NZ$2 million.

#### Do I have to run the business myself?

- Yes. The program requires you to manage and grow the business in New Zealand yourself, under the conditions of the visa.

#### Is there an English requirement?

- Yes. You need English at IELTS 5.0 or an equivalent certificate.

#### Is there a residence requirement in New Zealand?

- Yes. You need to live in New Zealand for at least 184 days a year.

#### What job requirements does the business have to meet?

- The business must maintain 5 full-time jobs and create 1 new job for a New Zealand citizen or permanent resident.

#### Can my family come with me?

- Yes. Your spouse and dependent children can be included. They need to meet the health, character, and documentation requirements that apply.

#### When can I apply for the Resident Visa?

- You can apply for a Resident Visa after 3 years on the NZ$1 million route, or after 12 months on the NZ$2 million route, provided the business meets all the program conditions.

#### Can I obtain New Zealand citizenship?

- It is possible. Once you hold the Resident Visa and have physically lived in New Zealand for the required 5 years, you can consider applying for citizenship.

#### What are the main risks?

- The risks usually lie in choosing an unsuitable business, incomplete financial and legal due diligence, failing to maintain the jobs, failing to create enough new jobs, not meeting the residence days, or a source of funds file that is not clear enough. This is a program that requires both an investment and hands-on management, so the outcome depends on your management ability, how the business performs, and the authorities' assessment.
