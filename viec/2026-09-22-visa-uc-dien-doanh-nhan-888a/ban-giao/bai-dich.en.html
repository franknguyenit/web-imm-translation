Australia 888A Business Innovation Visa

What is the 888A visa?

  • The 888A Business Innovation visa is Australia's permanent visa for investors who already hold a 188A visa in the Business Innovation stream.
  • Investors can move from the 188A provisional visa to 888A permanent residence once they have run the business and met the residence requirements in Australia.
  • A spouse or partner and dependent children can be granted the visa at the same time.
  • Once they hold the 888A and meet the residence requirements, investors can apply for Australian citizenship.
  • The 888A visa lets the investor continue to own and manage the Australian business as a permanent resident.

Requirements for moving from 188A to 888A

Ownership and operation of the business

  • Establish or acquire a business in Australia.
  • Run and manage that business directly.
  • Hold at least a 30% ownership stake in the business, or the stake prescribed where the business turnover exceeds the prescribed level.

Turnover and assets

  • Record annual turnover of at least A$300,000 (approximately US$214,000) in the Australian business in at least 1 year.
  • In the 12 months before lodgement, meet 2 of these 3 requirements: total personal and business assets in Australia at the prescribed level; total investment in the Australian business at the prescribed level; or at least 2 full-time jobs created for Australian citizens or permanent residents, not counting family members.

Residence in Australia

  • Meet the Australian residence requirement that applies to the subclass 888 visa at the time of lodgement.
  • IMM Group advises on the exact residence thresholds for your case, your nominating state or territory, and your lodgement date.

Compliance with the law

  • A clean criminal record, evidenced by a police clearance certificate.
  • No breaches of Australian law.

The 888A application process

Invest and trade in Australia

Start trading once the 188A visa is granted, then maintain the business, its turnover, its assets, and your residence in Australia in line with the requirements.

Lodge the 888A application

Assemble the evidence on the business, financial, and residence requirements, then lodge the 888A visa application.

Grant of 888A permanent residence

The 888A visa is granted where the application meets the requirements. Processing times depend on the individual case and on the Department of Home Affairs.

888A visa FAQs

How is the 888A visa different from the 188A?

188A versus 888A

  • The 188A is a provisional visa in the Business Innovation stream. The 888A is the permanent visa, for investors who have run a business and met the residence requirements in Australia.

What are the main requirements for the 888A?

Main requirements

  • The investor has to manage the Australian business directly, hold the required ownership stake, meet the turnover requirement, meet either the asset or the job-creation requirement, and complete the required period of residence.

Do I have to prove my source of funds?

Source of funds

  • Yes. Investors have to show that the invested capital is lawful and fully documented. How the source of funds is explained differs from case to case.

Can I buy an existing business in Australia?

Acquiring a business

  • Yes. The business you acquire should match your management experience and your business plan. Careful due diligence before the purchase matters, because a poorly chosen acquisition puts the application at risk.

Can the business change industry?

Changing industry

  • Adjustments are possible in the early stage where circumstances warrant. Before the 888A application, the business has to be trading steadily and meeting the requirements that apply.

Who has to meet the residence requirement?

Residence

  • The residence requirement can be met by either the applicant or the spouse or partner, as the applicable rules allow. Time spent in Australia by the applicant and by the spouse or partner cannot be added together.

Does the 888A visa include Medicare?

Medicare

  • Yes. Once the 888A is granted, the family is eligible for Medicare as Australian permanent residents. At the 188A stage, investors normally need private health cover.

Can I apply for Australian citizenship after the 888A?

A path to citizenship

  • Yes. Once they hold permanent residence and meet the residence, character, language, and citizenship test requirements, investors can lodge a citizenship application.
