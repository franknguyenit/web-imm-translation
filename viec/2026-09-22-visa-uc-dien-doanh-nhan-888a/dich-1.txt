[s003] What is the 888A visa?
[s004] The 888A Business Innovation visa is Australia's permanent visa for investors who already hold a 188A visa in the Business Innovation stream.
[s005] Investors move from the 188A provisional visa to 888A permanent residence once they have run the business and met the residence requirements in Australia.
[s006] A spouse or partner and dependent children can be granted the visa at the same time.
[s007] Once they hold the 888A and meet the residence requirements, investors can apply for Australian citizenship.
[s008] The 888A visa lets the investor go on owning and managing the Australian business as a permanent resident.

[s028] Requirements for moving from 188A to 888A
[s029] Ownership and operation of the business
[s030] Establish or acquire a business in Australia.
[s031] Run and manage that business directly.
[s032] Hold at least a 30% ownership stake in the business, or the stake set by the rules where the business turns over more than the prescribed threshold.
[s034] The Australian business records annual turnover of at least A$300,000 (approximately US$214,000) in at least 1 year.
[s033] Turnover and assets
[s035] In the 12 months before lodgement, meet 2 of these 3 requirements: total personal and business assets in Australia at the prescribed level; total investment in the Australian business at the prescribed level; or at least 2 full-time jobs created for Australian citizens or permanent residents, not counting family members.

[s070] 888A visa FAQs
[s071] How is the 888A visa different from the 188A?
[s072] The difference between the 188A and the 888A
[s073] The 188A is a provisional visa in the Business Innovation stream. The 888A is the permanent visa, for investors who have run a business and met the residence requirements in Australia.
[s074] What are the main requirements for the 888A?
[s075] Main requirements
[s076] The investor has to manage the Australian business directly, hold the required ownership stake, meet the turnover requirement, meet either the asset or the job-creation requirement, and complete the required period of residence.
[s077] Do I have to prove my source of funds?
[s078] Source of funds
[s079] Yes. Investors have to show that the invested capital is lawful and fully documented. Every case sets out its source of funds differently.
[s080] Can I buy an existing business in Australia?
[s081] Acquiring a business
[s082] Yes. The business you acquire should match your management experience and your business plan. Careful due diligence before the purchase matters, because a weak acquisition weakens the application.
[s083] Can the business change industry?
[s084] Changing industry
[s085] Adjustments are possible in the early stage where conditions on the ground call for them. Before the 888A application, the business has to be trading steadily and meeting the requirements set out in the rules.
[s086] Who has to meet the residence requirement?
[s087] Residence
[s088] The residence requirement can be met by either the applicant or the spouse or partner, as the rules allow. Time in Australia is not combined between the two.
[s089] Does the 888A visa include Medicare?
[s090] Medicare
[s091] Yes. Once the 888A is granted, the family is eligible for Medicare as Australian permanent residents. At the 188A stage, investors normally need private health cover.
[s092] Can I apply for Australian citizenship after the 888A?
[s093] A path to citizenship
[s094] Yes. Once they hold permanent residence and meet the residence, character, language, and citizenship test requirements, investors can lodge a citizenship application.
