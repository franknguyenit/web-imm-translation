# Portugal Golden Visa (Residency by Investment)

### What is the Portugal Golden Visa?

- The Portugal Golden Visa is a residency-by-investment program for investors who hold citizenship outside the European Union.
- You and your family receive a Portuguese residence permit once you meet the program's investment requirement.
- Your whole family has access to healthcare, education, and social security in Portugal on the terms set by law.
- As a Golden Visa holder, you and your family travel visa-free within the 29 countries of the Schengen Area.
- After 10 years of holding the Golden Visa and meeting the investment and residence conditions, you can file for Portuguese citizenship under the nationality rules in force at the time.
- The usual route is a fund investment from €500,000; some other routes start at €250,000.

## Benefits of the Portugal Golden Visa

### Live in Portugal

- Your whole family can live, work, and study in Portugal.

### Visa-free travel in the Schengen Area

- Travel across the 29 Schengen countries under the standard short-stay rule: 90 days in any 180-day period.

### The same benefits as local residents

- Access to Portugal's health and education systems on the same terms as local residents.

### A route to Portuguese citizenship

- You can apply for naturalization after 10 years if you meet the conditions.

### Once you hold Portuguese citizenship

- Visa-free or eVisa access to 189 countries, including the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, and other major countries.

### A route to the US E-2 visa

- Once you hold Portuguese citizenship and have at least 3 continuous years of residence in Portugal, you may be eligible to apply for a US E-2 investor visa.

### The right to live anywhere in the EU

- With Portuguese citizenship, your family has the right to live in any EU country.

## Why investors choose the Portugal Golden Visa

### A light physical residence requirement

- You need to spend an average of just 14 days in Portugal every 2 years to keep your Golden Visa
- Among the lighter stay requirements of the European programs.

### A clear route to citizenship

- You can be considered for citizenship after 10 years if you meet the conditions
- Among the European residency-by-investment programs with a more accessible route to citizenship.

### Flexible rules for dependent children

- One of the few programs that allows children of any age to be included
- The condition is that they are unmarried and financially dependent on you.

### Straightforward eligibility

- No age limit for applicants
- No interview
- No degree or management experience is required.

## Program requirements

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older, with no upper age limit
- Meets security and background vetting requirements
- You document a lawful source of funds for the investment

### Dependents you can include

- Your legal spouse
- Children, including adopted children, who are unmarried and financially dependent — with no age limit
- Parents of both spouses; those under 65 must show they are financially dependent on you
- Siblings under 18 who are financially dependent on you

### Investment routes:

- You choose one of the qualifying investment routes:
- Fund investment: €500,000 into a qualifying Portuguese investment fund, held for at least 5 years.
- A qualifying fund is registered in Portugal, has been running for more than 5 years, invests at least 60% of its capital in commercial companies based in Portugal, and does not invest directly or indirectly in real estate.
- Scientific research: an investment of at least €500,000.
- Arts and cultural heritage projects: a non-refundable donation of at least €250,000.
- Company formation: an investment of at least €500,000 that creates 5 jobs, or forming a new company that creates 10 jobs.
- Real estate: the property ownership route, which started at €280,000, has been closed since October 2023.

## Portugal Golden Visa FAQs

#### How is the investment structured?

The Portugal Golden Visa has several qualifying investment routes

- Fund investment — from €500,000, the route most investors take today.
- Scientific research — from €500,000.
- Arts and cultural heritage — a non-refundable donation from €250,000.
- Forming or investing in a company — from €500,000, with job creation conditions.
- The real estate route has been closed since October 2023. The amounts and conditions that apply are those in force when you apply. (Updated: June 2026.)

#### What is a Golden Visa, and how does it differ from permanent residence?

What the Golden Visa is, and how it differs from permanent residence

- The Portugal Golden Visa is a long-term residence permit for investors. In day-to-day terms it gives your family much of what permanent residence does: you can live, work, start a business, and put your children through school in Portugal.
- The difference: the Golden Visa runs for a fixed term and has to be renewed periodically. After 10 years of residence, you can be considered for citizenship if you qualify.

#### What about Schengen and the EU?

Short stays in the Schengen Area — not a right to live or work elsewhere in the EU

- The Golden Visa allows travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules of 90 days in any 180-day period.
- Travel to EU countries outside the Schengen Area still requires a visitor visa, though such applications are usually given priority.
- The right to live, work, and study in any EU country arises only once you hold Portuguese citizenship.

#### Schengen is not the same as the EU

The EU — the European Union: an intergovernmental organization of 27 countries.

- The Schengen Area is a zone where you can travel freely between the 29 member countries.
- Some EU countries are not in Schengen, and the reverse is also true. Portugal is a member of both.
- So a Golden Visa already allows short trips within the Schengen Area; the right to live and work across the EU arises only with citizenship.

#### Do my children study free of charge?

Free schooling for your children

- While your family holds a valid Golden Visa, your children attend Portugal's public schools free of charge, from primary through the end of secondary school.
- At university level, tuition and any support available depend on the institution and on your child's residence status at enrollment.

#### Is income earned outside Portugal taxed?

Tax on income earned outside Portugal

- Portuguese income tax applies only once you become a tax resident — generally when you spend more than 183 days a year in Portugal.
- Investors who visit only as long as they need to in order to keep or renew the permit usually fall outside that.
- Tax policy can change, and your own position should be confirmed with a tax advisor at the time you apply. (Updated: June 2026.)

#### What are my options after 5 years?

Your options after 5 years

- After 5 years of holding the Golden Visa you have 3 options: keep renewing the residence permit; apply for permanent residence
- If you keep renewing, you have to maintain the investment. If you apply for permanent residence or citizenship, once you qualify, you are no longer required to keep the investment.

#### What are the requirements for citizenship?

To be considered for Portuguese citizenship you need

- To be 18 or older;
- To have held a valid Golden Visa for at least 10 years, meeting the stay requirement of an average of 14 days every 2 years;
- To reach level A2 in Portuguese;
- And to have no criminal record in Portugal or abroad.
- Applications are assessed under the rules in force at the time you apply.

#### What is the A2 Portuguese exam?

The language requirement is a Portuguese certificate at CEFR level A2.

- The exam can be run by an accredited school in Portugal or in another Portuguese-speaking country, by IEFP, Portugal's public employment and training institute, or by an accredited test center.
- A2 is a basic level; comparisons with IELTS are indicative only.

#### Is an online certificate accepted?

Online certificates

- Under the rules, you and your family members need a certificate of completion from a Portuguese course at a public school in Portugal, or at a licensed center in Portugal.
- Whether an online course is accepted depends on the rules in force — check before you enroll.

#### What does Portuguese citizenship give you?

What citizenship gives you

- Full Portuguese citizenship rights, including the right to live, work, and study in any EU country.
- The Portuguese passport is among the stronger ones, with visa-free or eVisa access to a wide range of countries (the number changes over time).
- Once you hold Portuguese citizenship and have 3 or more continuous years of residence in Portugal, you can explore the US E-2 route under the E-2 rules in force at the time.

#### What are the investment risks?

The investment risks

- The two main risks: choosing a project or fund without a solid track record, or one that does not qualify under the program; and investment capital from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.
- IMM Group reviews your file and the project in detail before any contract is signed, to limit these risks.

Note: a fund investment carries risk; returns and the recovery of your capital depend on how the fund performs.

#### What source of funds evidence is required?

Documenting your source of funds

- Documenting your source of funds for the Portugal Golden Visa is generally simpler than for programs such as the United States, Australia, or Canada — you show a lawful source at a basic level of detail, for example a property sale, income, or a business.
- Every family documents this differently — contact IMM Group for advice on your own situation.

#### How long is the permit valid, and how is it renewed?

Validity and renewal

- The first Golden Visa permit runs for 2 years and can be renewed, usually for a further 3 years in the next cycle, provided you still hold the investment and have a clean criminal record.
- After 5 years, if you qualify, you can apply for permanent residence. The validity and renewal cycles follow the rules in force at the time of renewal.

#### Is there an age limit for applicants?

The Portugal Golden Visa sets no upper age limit for the applicant.

- You need only be 18 or older and meet the requirements on background checks and a lawful source of funds.

#### Are qualifications or language skills required?

Qualifications and language

- At the investment stage the program requires no qualifications, no management experience, no proof of language ability, and no interview.
- The requirement for Portuguese at level A2 applies later, at the citizenship stage.

#### Which family members can be included?

The Golden Visa lets you include a multigenerational family in a single application

- Your legal spouse;
- Children, including adopted children, who are unmarried and financially dependent;
- Parents of both spouses, where those under 65 show they are financially dependent on you;
- And siblings under 18 who are financially dependent on you.

#### Are adopted children and stepchildren eligible?

The program allows adopted children and stepchildren to be included in their parents' application.

- An adopted child needs a legal adoption certificate.
- A stepchild needs legal documents plus a notarized letter of consent from the spouse allowing the child to join the application.
- IMM Group assists with preparing the documents.

#### What is the age limit for dependent children?

The age limit for dependent children

- The program sets no hard upper age limit for dependent children — a child can be included if they are unmarried and financially dependent on you, which usually means they are in full-time study.
- This is one of the advantages of the Portugal Golden Visa over many other programs. The exact conditions follow the rules in force.

#### Can parents be included?

Parents in the application

- Parents of both you and your spouse can be included. Parents aged 65 or older can join
- Those under 65 need to show they are financially dependent on you.
- The exact conditions follow the rules in force when you apply.

#### How many days a year must I spend in Portugal?

The Portugal Golden Visa does not require continuous residence.

- To keep and renew the permit you need only meet the minimum stay requirement — an average of about 7 days a year, or 14 days every 2 years.
- If you plan to apply for citizenship later, you will also need to meet the residence, language, and integration conditions set by law.

#### Can I sell or exit the investment after 5 years?

Selling or exiting after 5 years

- The real estate route has been closed since October 2023, so investors now qualify mainly through a fund.
- You need to hold the fund investment continuously for at least 5 years.

#### Can I switch to a different fund?

Switching your fund investment

- You need to hold a qualifying investment continuously throughout the required period.
- Any switch between funds must keep the holding period unbroken and follow the program rules.
- Questions about buying or selling real estate no longer apply, because that route is closed.
