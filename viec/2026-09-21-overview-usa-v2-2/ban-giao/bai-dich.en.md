## Filing deadline for Form I-526E:

September 30, 2026

For EB-5 investors, the timing of your filing is key to managing costs and legal risk. The **September 30, 2026** date directly affects the **US$800,000 investment amount**, your legal rights, and the stability of your petition.

! Grandfathering — protection for filed petitions

Under the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022 (RIA), only **Form I-526E** petitions filed by **September 30, 2026** are grandfathered under the current requirements if the program is changed later.

Each program suits a different goal, budget and business model for investors and entrepreneurs who want to settle in the United States. They fall into 2 groups: investing directly for a green card, and immigrating as a business owner.

Purpose

- Move to the United States to run and work in your subsidiary
- Give your children a U.S. education

How you invest

Set up a U.S. subsidiary and transfer an executive or senior manager from the parent company abroad to run it.

Company requirements

- Parent company abroad: substantial in size, with the financial capacity to invest in the United States
- U.S. company: a subsidiary or affiliate, large enough to require an executive or senior manager to run it

Investor requirements

- Currently employed in a managerial or executive capacity at the parent company abroad
- Has the capability and experience to run the U.S. subsidiary

Key benefits

Very fast — as little as 15 business days with premium processing

Your whole family can live, study and work in the United States

Free public schooling through high school for children under 21

After 1 year of successful operations, you may apply for EB-1C — a route to a green card and U.S. citizenship

Points to consider

You must show the business is trading well from year one to qualify for an extension

Visa valid 1–3 years, up to 7 years in total — renewal depends on how the business performs

Suits families who want to

- Keep a U.S. base without settling permanently

How you invest

Invest in a new or existing U.S. business.

Special condition

You must hold citizenship of an E-2 treaty country: Grenada, Türkiye (Turkey), Montenegro and others.

If you obtained that citizenship by investment, you must also have been domiciled in that country for at least 3 continuous years before applying for E-2

Key benefits

You, your spouse and your children under 21 can work, do business and study in the United States

Own a U.S. business and draw its profits

Whether you pay U.S. tax on worldwide income depends on how long you spend in the United States — confirm your position with an independent tax advisor

Free public schooling for your children through high school

Points to consider

Requires citizenship of an E-2 treaty country and, where that citizenship was obtained by investment, 3 years of domicile there

Visa valid 2–5 years — renewal depends on how the U.S. business performs
