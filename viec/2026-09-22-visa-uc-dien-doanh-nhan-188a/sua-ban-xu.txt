[s004] The 188A Business Innovation visa was the Business Innovation stream of Australia's Business Innovation and Investment Program, for experienced business owners establishing, acquiring, or franchising a business in Australia.
[s005] The investment requirement started at A$300,000 and varied with the requirements of each state or territory.
[s025] The investor decides how the business is run, who it hires, and the direction it takes.
[s027] With a threshold from A$300,000, the investment requirement was among the lowest of Australia's business and investor visa streams.
[s032] Aged 18–55.
[s035] Ownership of a business, or a shareholding carrying an active management role.
[s053] Once a state or territory nomination was granted, the 188A visa application was lodged.
[s055] The Department of Home Affairs assessed the application against the visa criteria.
[s057] The applicant and accompanying family members completed the required health examinations.
[s089] Applicants needed at least 65 points. The score was calculated from age, English language ability, qualifications, experience, financial position, business turnover, and related factors.
[s095] Real estate, business assets, savings accounts, shareholdings, and assets held in the name of the applicant or their spouse or partner could all be counted.
[s098] Adjustments are possible in the early stage where commercial circumstances call for them. The business still has to be stable and meet the requirements when the 888A permanent visa application is made.
[s104] The 188A is a provisional visa, so private health insurance is normally required. Medicare eligibility comes with permanent residence.
