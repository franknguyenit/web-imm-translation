# Brief — Visa Uc dien doanh nhan 188A (Business Innovation stream)

- Muc dich trang: trang dich vu gioi thieu mot dien visa. Chuong trinh DA DONG voi ho so moi tu 31/07/2024 (BIIP closed permanently — immi.homeaffairs.gov.au xac nhan nguyen van). Nguoi doc thuc te: nguoi da nop ho so 188A truoc moc do va dang chuan bi len 888A, hoac nguoi tra cuu xem con mo khong.
- Nguoi doc dich: chu doanh nghiep quoc te, 18-55 tuoi, dang giu visa 188A hoac da nop; gia dinh co con duoi 23 tuoi.
- Thi truong hop: nguoi da o Uc hoac sap sang Uc; tim kiem bang tieng Anh o AU, SG, UK, CA.
- Giong: co van diem tinh, ngoi THU BA ("the investor", "applicants", "188A visa holders"). KHONG dung "you/your" — noi "your business plan" la moi nguoi doc vao dien khong con nop duoc (bai hoc trang Ontario/Saskatchewan).
- Thi: QUA KHU cho dieu kien tham gia, quy trinh nop, bao lanh bang. HIEN TAI cho dieu con dung voi nguoi dang giu visa 188A (quyen loi gia dinh, hoc hanh, di lai) va cho lo trinh 888A van mo. Day la lua chon CO Y — agent soat dung bat "sai thi".
- Dua dinh dong chuong trinh len TRUOC loi moi trong khoi luu y va vao meta description.
- Phan doan: dich SAT dieu kien / so tien / quy trinh / cau hoi dap. VIET LAI H1, khoi mo dau, tieu de muc, CTA, gach dau dong loi ich.
- Bo ngu canh Viet: s016 "giua Viet Nam va Uc" -> "between Australia and their home country"; s036 "kinh nghiem kinh doanh thanh cong tai Viet Nam" -> "in their home country"; s038 tieu de "Dieu kien doanh nghiep tai Viet Nam" -> "Requirements for the existing business"; s081 "Nhung nguoi Viet thanh cong tren dat Uc" -> "Immigrant success stories in Australia".
- Sua ten goi sai cua nguon (ghi bao cao, khong tu sua ban Viet): "tinh bang Uc" -> Uc co STATE va TERRITORY, khong co province; "Bo Di tru Uc" -> Department of Home Affairs; "Thang diem Di tru Uc" -> points test (KHONG dung "selection factor grid" — do la ten cua New Brunswick trong thuat-ngu.csv, can co #bo-qua-tn).
- Thuat ngu chot phai giu: "chung minh nguon tien" -> source of funds; "thuong tru" -> permanent residence.
- Tien: A$ (style guide muc 5), giu nguyen te AUD, khong quy doi sang USD.
- Tu khoa chinh: **188A Business Innovation visa** (doi thu dat tieu de theo ma subclass + ten stream chinh thuc: "Subclass 188A - Business Innovation Stream", "Australia 188A Business Innovation Visa: Requirements & Guide"). Nguoi tim go MA DIEN, khong go "golden visa" — giong mau PNP Canada.
- Tu khoa phu: subclass 188A requirements · Australia business visa for entrepreneurs · 888A permanent visa · state or territory nomination · BIIP closure · Australia business migration.
