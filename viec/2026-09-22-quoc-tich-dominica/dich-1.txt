[s003] What is Dominica citizenship by investment?
[s004] Dominica citizenship by investment is a program run by the Government of Dominica, in place since 1993.
[s005] You can choose between a contribution to the Economic Diversification Fund and an investment in a government-approved real estate project.
[s006] Once you hold citizenship, you have visa-free access to 143 countries worldwide.
[s007] One application can cover 3 generations: your spouse, dependent children, and dependent parents over 65.
[s008] Processing takes 8–10 months.
[s009] The holding period is short — just 3 years.
[s010] The Financial Times rated Dominica the world's best second citizenship option for 4 consecutive years, 2017–2020.

[s012] Benefits of Dominican citizenship
[s013] Global mobility
[s014] Visa-free or eVisa access to 143 countries.
[s015] A helpful basis for US and Canadian visa applications
[s016] A Dominican passport can make applying for US and Canadian visas more straightforward, though each application is decided by the authorities of that country.
[s017] No tax on worldwide income
[s018] Dominica levies no personal or corporate income tax on income arising anywhere in the world; how your income is taxed still depends on where you are tax resident, so confirm your own position with an independent tax advisor.
[s019] No tax on wealth
[s020] No property tax, no inheritance tax, and no gift tax.
[s021] Flexibility over the name on your passport
[s022] You can take a new name when you naturalize or keep your current one. If you take a new name, your Dominican passport does not refer to the former one, which some investors find easier in international business.
[s023] Citizenship for life
[s024] Dominican citizenship is held for life and can be passed on to later generations under the country's rules.

[s081] What are the investment risks?
[s082] The two main risks
[s083] Choosing a project or developer without a solid track record, or a project that does not qualify.
[s084] Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.

[s086] When was the program established?
[s087] Established in 1993
[s088] One of the oldest and most respected economic citizenship programs in the Caribbean
[s089] Ranked highly by the CBI Index from 2017 to 2020.
[s090] Contribution or real estate: which route?
[s091] The two routes
[s092] Contribution to the EDF: from US$200,000 for a single applicant, or US$250,000 for a family of up to 4; non-refundable.
[s093] Real estate: from US$200,000, held for 3 years, after which it can be transferred.
[s094] Each route has its advantages; IMM Group advises on which one fits your file.
[s095] How long must the real estate investment be held?
[s096] The holding period
[s097] The real estate investment must be held for at least 3 years, with no periodic filing to make during that time.
[s098] After 3 years you may transfer your share; the price you achieve depends on the market and on the project terms.
[s099] Is there a penalty for selling the property?
[s100] Selling before the 3 years are up can cost you your citizenship
[s101] After 3 years you may transfer the investment without affecting your citizenship.
[s102] What about tax on a sale, and taxes on assets?
[s103] Tax on a sale, and taxes on assets
[s104] Dominica levies no capital gains tax, property tax, inheritance tax, gift tax, or tax on foreign income under its current rules.
[s105] Is there an age limit or an education requirement?
[s106] Age limits and qualifications
[s107] There is no age limit; no qualifications or experience are required, and there is no interview.
[s108] Residence requirements
[s109] Residence requirements
[s110] You do not need to travel to Dominica while your application is processed, and there is no residence requirement once you hold citizenship.
[s111] What do you receive on naturalization?
[s112] The whole family receives citizenship for life, which can be passed on to later generations.
[s113] The passport gives visa-free or eVisa access to more than 140 countries, and can make US and Canadian visa applications more straightforward.
[s114] How long is the passport valid?
[s115] Passport validity
[s116] The Dominican passport is valid for 10 years and is renewed under the country's rules.
[s117] Up to what age can children be included?
[s118] Dependent children
[s119] Unmarried, financially dependent children under 30 can be included in the application.
[s120] Can parents be included?
[s121] Parents in the application
[s122] Parents on both sides aged 65 or older can be included in the same application.

[s124] The nature island of the Caribbean.

[s126] Home to many rare plants, animals, and birds.
[s127] An ecosystem with 365 rivers, along with waterfalls and hot springs fed in part by volcanic activity.
[s128] A paradise for divers, hikers, and anyone who loves the outdoors.
[s129] Warm all year round, averaging 29°C.
[s130] The dry season runs from December to May and the wet season from June to November.
[s131] Summers are pleasant, with varying rainfall that sustains the island's diverse ecosystem.
[s132] The official currency is the East Caribbean dollar (XCD).
[s133] The main language is English, and French Creole, known locally as Patois, is also widely spoken.
[s134] Getting around Dominica is safe and easy by car, taxi, bus, or boat, with fixed taxi fares on most routes.
