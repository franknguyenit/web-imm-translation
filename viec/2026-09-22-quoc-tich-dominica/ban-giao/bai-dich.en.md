# Dominica Citizenship by Investment

### What is Dominica citizenship by investment?

- Dominica citizenship by investment is a program run by the Government of Dominica, in place since 1993.
- You can choose between a contribution to the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) and an investment in a government-approved real estate project.
- Once you hold citizenship, you have visa-free or eVisa access to 143 destinations under current visa agreements.
- One application can cover 3 generations: your spouse, dependent children, and dependent parents over 65.
- Processing takes 8–10 months.
- The holding period is short — just 3 years.
- The Financial Times rated Dominica the world's best second citizenship option for 4 consecutive years, 2017–2020.

## Benefits of Dominica citizenship

### Global mobility

- Visa-free or eVisa access to 143 destinations under current visa agreements.

### An additional basis for US and Canadian visa applications

- Holding a Dominica passport gives you an additional basis on which to apply for US and Canadian visas; each application is decided on its own merits by the authorities of that country.

### Dominica does not tax worldwide income

- Dominica levies no personal or corporate income tax on income arising anywhere in the world. How your own income is taxed still depends on where you are tax resident, so confirm your position with an independent tax advisor.

### No tax on wealth

- No property tax, no inheritance tax, and no gift tax under Dominica's current rules.

### Flexibility over the name on your passport

- You can take a new name when you naturalize or keep your current one. If you take a new name, the Dominica passport records only that name.

### Citizenship for life

- Dominica citizenship is held for life and can be passed on to later generations under the country's rules.

## Dominica citizenship requirements

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older
- No upper age limit
- Meets background security and medical requirements
- Can document a lawful source of funds

### Dependents you can include

- Your spouse
- Children under 30 who are unmarried and financially dependent
- Parents of the applicant and spouse who are over 65
- Grandparents of the applicant and spouse
- All dependents must also meet the background security and medical requirements

### The contribution route

- A contribution to the Economic Diversification Fund
- US$250,000 for a family of up to 4
- US$200,000 for a single applicant

### The real estate route

- An investment of at least US$200,000
- In a government-approved real estate project
- Held for 3 years
- Example project: Secret Bay, a luxury resort in Dominica

## Dominica citizenship by investment FAQs

#### What are the investment risks?

The 2 main risks

- Choosing a project or developer without a solid track record, or a project that does not qualify.
- Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.

IMM Group conducts detailed due diligence on the project and your file before any contract is signed, to limit these risks.

#### When was the program established?

Established in 1993

- One of the oldest and best-established citizenship by investment programs in the Caribbean
- Ranked highly by the CBI Index from 2017 to 2020.

#### Contribution or real estate: which route?

The 2 routes

- Contribution to the EDF: from US$200,000 for a single applicant, or US$250,000 for a family of up to 4; non-refundable.
- Real estate: from US$200,000, held for 3 years, after which it can be transferred.

Each route has its advantages; IMM Group advises on which one fits your file.

#### How long must the real estate investment be held?

The holding period

- The real estate investment must be held for at least 3 years, with no periodic reporting during that time.
- After 3 years you may transfer your share; the price you achieve depends on the market and on the project terms.

#### Is there a penalty for selling the property?

Selling before the 3-year holding period ends can cost you your citizenship

- After 3 years you may transfer the investment without affecting your citizenship.

#### Is there tax on a sale, or tax on assets?

Tax on a sale and on assets

- As its rules currently stand, Dominica levies no capital gains tax, property tax, inheritance tax, gift tax, or tax on foreign income.

#### Is there an age limit or an education requirement?

Age limits and qualifications

- There is no age limit, no education or experience requirement, and no interview.

#### Residence requirements

Residence requirements

- You do not need to travel to Dominica while your application is processed, and there is no residence requirement once you hold citizenship.

#### What do you receive on naturalization?

The whole family receives citizenship for life, which can be passed on to later generations.

- The passport gives visa-free or eVisa access to more than 140 destinations, and gives you an additional basis on which to apply for US and Canadian visas.

#### How long is the passport valid?

Passport validity

- The Dominica passport is valid for 10 years and can be renewed under the country's rules.

#### Up to what age can children be included?

Dependent children

- Unmarried, financially dependent children under 30 can be included in the application.

#### Can parents be included?

Parents in the application

- Parents on both sides aged 65 or older can be included in the same application.

#### What is Dominica like?

The Nature Island of the Caribbean.

- More than two-thirds of the island is covered in rainforest, running from north to south.
- Home to many rare plants, animals, and birds.
- An ecosystem with 365 rivers, plus waterfalls and hot springs fed in part by volcanic activity.
- A paradise for divers, hikers, and anyone who loves the outdoors.
- Warm all year round, averaging 29°C (84°F).
- The dry season runs from December to May and the wet season from June to November.
- Summers are pleasant, with varying rainfall that sustains the island's diverse ecosystem.
- The official currency is the East Caribbean dollar (XCD).
- The main language is English, and French Creole, known locally as Patois, is also widely spoken.
- Getting around Dominica is safe and easy by car, taxi, bus, or boat, with fixed taxi fares on most routes.
