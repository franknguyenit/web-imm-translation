[s003] What is St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship by investment?
[s004] St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship by investment is a program run by the Government of St. Kitts and Nevis.
[s005] You can choose between a contribution to the Sustainable Island State Contribution fund and an investment in a government-approved real estate project.
[s006] Once you hold citizenship, you have visa-free access to 155 countries, including Europe and the Schengen Area.
[s007] St. Kitts and Nevis runs one of the oldest citizenship by investment programs in the world.

[s010] Benefits of St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship
[s011] Global mobility
[s012] Visa-free or eVisa access to 155 countries, including the United Kingdom, most of Europe, the Schengen Area, South Korea, and Hong Kong.
[s013] No tax on worldwide income
[s014] St. Kitts and Nevis levies no personal or corporate income tax on income arising anywhere in the world; how your income is taxed still depends on where you are tax resident, so confirm your own position with an independent tax advisor.
[s015] No tax on wealth
[s016] No property tax, no inheritance tax, and no gift tax.
[s017] Financial privacy
[s018] Local law protects the confidentiality of investors' bank account information in St. Kitts and Nevis.
[s019] Visa-free travel to the United Kingdom
[s020] The whole family can enter the United Kingdom without a visa for tourism, family visits, business, or transit, for stays of up to 6 months at a time.

[s069] St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship by investment FAQs
[s070] What are the investment risks?
[s071] The two main risks
[s072] Choosing a project or developer without a solid track record, or a project that does not qualify.
[s073] Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.
[s074] IMM Group reviews the project and your file in detail before any contract is signed, to limit these risks.
[s075] Contribution or real estate: which route?
[s076] The two routes
[s077] Contribution to the fund: from US$250,000 for a family of up to 4, non-refundable.
[s078] Real estate: from US$325,000, held for 7 years; you may receive a share of profits under the project terms and can transfer the investment after 7 years.
[s079] IMM Group advises on which route fits your file.
[s080] What happens to the real estate payment if my application is refused?
[s081] On the real estate route you first pay a deposit of part of the price, typically 5–10%.
[s082] If your application is refused, the developer returns that deposit; government filing, processing, and due diligence fees already paid are generally not refunded.
[s083] Age, qualifications, and interview?
[s084] There is no age limit, and no qualifications, experience, or language skills are required.
[s085] The program does require an interview.
[s086] Residence requirements
[s087] You do not need to travel to St. Kitts and Nevis while your application is processed.
[s088] There is no residence requirement once you hold citizenship.
[s089] Is investing in Park Hyatt St. Kitts safe?
[s090] What the Park Hyatt investment involves
[s091] Park Hyatt St. Kitts has been open since November 2017, so there is no construction completion risk left.
[s092] The Park Hyatt brand and steady visitor numbers support the hotel's operation, though returns are not guaranteed.
[s093] How long must the property be held, and can it be resold?
[s094] The real estate investment must be held for at least 7 years.
[s095] After 7 years you may transfer it; the price you achieve depends on the market and on the project terms.
[s096] Can citizenship be revoked?
[s097] Citizenship is not withdrawn where you hold the investment for the required period.
[s098] Citizenship is held for life and can be passed on to later generations under the country's rules.

[s102] Can parents be included?
[s103] Parents in the application
