St. Kitts and Nevis Citizenship by Investment

What is St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship by investment?

  • St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship by investment is a program established and run by the country's government.
  • You can choose between a contribution to the Sustainable Growth Fund and an investment in a government-approved real estate project.
  • Once you hold citizenship, you have visa-free access to 155 countries and territories, including much of Europe and the Schengen Area.
  • St. Kitts and Nevis runs one of the oldest citizenship by investment programs in the world.
  • IMM Group is a Vietnam-based firm licensed by the Government of St. Kitts and Nevis as an authorized marketing agent for the program.

Benefits of St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship

Global mobility

  • Visa-free or eVisa access to 155 countries, including the United Kingdom, most of Europe, the Schengen Area, South Korea, and Hong Kong.

No tax on worldwide income

  • St. Kitts and Nevis levies no personal or corporate income tax on income arising anywhere in the world. How your income is taxed overall still depends on where you are tax resident, so confirm your own position with an independent tax advisor.

No property, inheritance, or gift tax

  • No property tax, no inheritance tax, and no gift tax.

Financial privacy

  • Local law protects the confidentiality of bank account information held in St. Kitts and Nevis.

Visa-free travel to the United Kingdom

  • The whole family can enter the United Kingdom without a visa for tourism, family visits, business, or transit, for stays of up to 6 months at a time.

A long-established, stable program

St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship requirements

Applicant requirements

  • Aged 18 or older
  • No upper age limit
  • Meets background security and medical requirements
  • Documents a lawful source of the investment funds

Dependents you can include

  • Your spouse
  • Children under 18
  • Children aged 18–25 who are in education and financially dependent
  • Parents of the applicant or spouse aged 55 or older who are financially dependent
  • All dependents must also meet the background security and medical requirements

The contribution route

  • A contribution to the Sustainable Growth Fund
  • US$250,000 for a family of 4
  • The contribution is non-refundable

The real estate route

  • An investment of at least US$325,000
  • In a government-approved real estate project
  • Held for 7 years
  • Example project: Park Hyatt St. Kitts, a 5-star hotel in operation since 2017

St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship by investment FAQs

What are the investment risks?

The two main risks

  • Choosing a project or developer without a solid track record, or a project that does not qualify.
  • Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.

IMM Group reviews the project and your file in detail before any contract is signed, to limit these risks.

Contribution or real estate: which route?

The two routes

  • Sustainable Growth Fund contribution: from US$250,000 for a family of up to 4, non-refundable.
  • Real estate: from US$325,000, held for 7 years; you may receive a share of profits under the project terms and can transfer the investment after 7 years.

IMM Group advises on which route fits your file.

What happens to the real estate payment if my application is refused?

On the real estate route, you first pay a deposit of 5–10% of the purchase price.

  • If your application is refused, the developer returns that deposit; government filing, processing, and due diligence fees already paid are generally not refunded.

Age, qualifications, and interview?

There is no age limit, and no qualifications, experience, or language skills are required.

  • The program does require an interview.

Residence requirements

You do not need to travel to St. Kitts and Nevis while your application is processed.

  • There is no residence requirement once you hold citizenship.

Is investing in Park Hyatt St. Kitts safe?

What the Park Hyatt investment involves

  • Park Hyatt St. Kitts has been open since November 2017, so it no longer carries the construction completion risk that applies to projects still being built.
  • The Park Hyatt brand and steady visitor numbers support the hotel's operation.

How long must the property be held, and can it be resold?

The real estate investment must be held for at least 7 years.

  • After 7 years you may transfer it; the price you achieve will depend on market conditions and the project terms.

Can citizenship be revoked?

Citizenship is not revoked on investment grounds as long as you hold the investment for the required period.

  • Citizenship is held for life and can be passed on to later generations under the country's rules.

Up to what age can children be included?

Dependent children

  • Under the 2025 rules, dependent children under 30 can be included in the application

Can parents be included?

Parents in the application

  • Parents on both sides aged 55 or older can be included in the application.
