## Ecoplastic: an EB-5 project offered exclusively through IMM Group in Vietnam

#### Ecoplastic auto parts plant: project overview

- Manufactures interior and exterior auto parts such as bumpers, center consoles, body panels, and front trunks (frunks) for electric vehicles.
- Technology: injection molding and robotic automated painting.
- Supplies 3 Hyundai-Kia plants in the U.S.: Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America (HMGMA), Kia Georgia, and Hyundai Alabama.
- Serves electric and hybrid models including the Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 9, and Palisade, and the Kia EV9 and Sportage.

#### Phase 2: expanding the manufacturing campus

- Not a project on paper: Phase 1 is complete and already in production in Georgia.
- Phase 2 is being built next door, adding capacity in line with Hyundai and Kia's production growth plans.
- The two plants share a common management system and operating chain.
- Seamless supply to Hyundai-Kia assembly lines in the U.S.

#### Advanced just-in-time (JIT) and just-in-sequence (JIS) production

- Hyundai's system sends a signal when a vehicle enters the assembly line.
- Ecoplastic prepares parts matched to each vehicle's exact configuration within a 180-minute window (about 3 hours).
- Parts are delivered directly to the assembly point at the Hyundai/Kia plant.
- Synchronized with the supply chain to minimize inventory time and keep production on schedule.

## Structural features designed to manage EB-5 investment risk

### 3 layers of repayment guarantees on the EB-5 loan

#### Layer 1: Ecoplastic Corporation (KOSDAQ: 038110)

- A publicly listed Korean company and a Tier 1 Hyundai-Kia supplier for more than 40 years.
- Provides a guarantee covering repayment of 100% of the EB-5 loan.
- Committed to maintaining tangible net worth of at least US$200 million for the full term of the loan.
- 2024 revenue: approximately US$1.69 billion.

#### Layer 2: the parent company that owns the U.S. project company

- Provides a guarantee covering repayment of 100% of the EB-5 loan.

#### Layer 3: Seojin Automotive (KOSDAQ: 122690)

- A major shareholder of Ecoplastic Corporation (about 31.65% ownership).
- Guarantees repayment of the EB-5 loan, liable for an amount equal to 31.65% of the total outstanding EB-5 loan balance.

### Collateral securing the EB-5 loan

#### Real estate collateral

- A first-position lien on the leasehold interest in the land and the Phase 2 plant buildings.
- Takes effect once EB-5 disbursements reach US$20 million.

#### Machinery and equipment collateral

- A first-position lien on all machinery and equipment at the Phase 2 plant.
- Takes effect once EB-5 disbursements reach US$40 million.

#### Financial covenants

- From 2030, the project must maintain a cash balance of more than US$40 million set aside for EB-5 loan repayment.
- From 2031, this balance must exceed the total outstanding principal of the EB-5 loan.
- No principal may be repaid to the parent company unless the reserve and liquidity conditions for the EB-5 loan have been met.
