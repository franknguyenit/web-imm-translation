Australian Permanent Residence — 888C Significant Investor Visa

What is the 888C visa?

  • The 888C Significant Investor visa is Australia's permanent residence (PR) visa for investors who hold an eligible 188C visa.
  • The whole family — spouse or partner and dependent children — can be granted PR at the same time.
  • No further investment is required.
  • Once they hold the 888C and meet the residence requirements, the family can move toward Australian citizenship.
  • The 888C is the permanent residence step for investors who already hold a 188C; the 188/888 program (BIIP) has stopped accepting new expressions of interest.

Benefits of Australian permanent residence

Live, study, work, and do business in Australia

  • Freedom to live, study, work, and run a business in any state or territory of Australia.

The rights of a permanent resident

  • Medicare, social security, and public education are available, subject to the residence conditions, once the 888C is granted.

Freedom to travel in and out of Australia

  • Travel in and out of Australia freely as a permanent resident.

A path to Australian citizenship

  • A path to Australian citizenship opens once the physical residence requirements and the other citizenship criteria are met; citizenship is assessed separately from PR.

Advantages of the 888C visa

PR for the whole family

  • Every eligible family member can be granted Australian permanent residence.

No further investment required

  • Once the requirements carried over from the 188C visa are met, investors do not have to invest again to apply for the 888C.

A flexible residence requirement

  • The applicant needs to spend 40 days or more a year in Australia, or the spouse or partner 180 days or more a year, while holding the 188C.

No points test

  • The 888C is the transition step for investors who already hold an eligible 188C, not a new points-tested application.

Requirements for 888C permanent residence

Maintain the investment

  • Keep the investment made under the 188C visa in place up to the date the 888C is lodged.

Meet the residence requirement

  • The applicant needs to spend 40 days or more a year in Australia, or the spouse or partner 180 days or more a year.
  • Time spent in Australia by the applicant and by the spouse or partner cannot be added together.

Compliance with the law

  • Compliance with the law and a clean criminal record, evidenced by a police clearance certificate.

A commitment to Australia

  • An intention to continue investing or doing business in Australia (the Commitment to State).

The 888C application process

Maintain the 188C investment and meet the residence requirement

Keep the investment in place under the 188C program and meet the residence requirement.

Prepare and lodge the 888C application

Assemble the evidence on the investment and residence requirements, then lodge the 888C visa application.

Grant of the 888C visa

You receive the 888C visa, which grants permanent residence, once the application is approved.

888C visa FAQs

How is the 888C different from the 188A and the 188B?

The main differences

  • The 188C and 888C streams have no age limit, no points test, and a flexible residence requirement (40 days per year). Processing times depend on the individual case and on current policy. The trade-off is a higher level of investment than for the 188A and the 188B.

What are the investment risks?

Key risks

  • A fund investment is an at-risk investment: its value can move with the performance of the fund.
  • Application risk comes from choosing a fund that does not meet the standard, from a source-of-funds explanation that is not fully documented, or from an advisor without the right experience.
  • IMM Group reviews the fund and the file in detail before any contract is signed, to help manage that risk.

Is the investor still eligible for the 888C if the fund loses money?

The effect of a loss-making fund

  • Yes. Where the investment falls in value because of how the fund performed rather than because capital was withdrawn, the investor remains eligible for the 888C, provided the investment was held for the required period and no capital was withdrawn early.

The investment is at risk.

What source of funds evidence is required?

Source of funds

  • Investors have to document a lawful, clean source of funds, to the same standard as in the U.S. and Canada.
  • Eligible assets include real estate, business assets, savings, and shareholdings or listed shares — held in the name of the applicant and/or the spouse or partner.
  • The path of funds has to be explained clearly and backed by documents.

Can capital be withdrawn after the 888C is lodged?

Withdrawing the capital

  • Yes, once the investment has been held for the required period and the 888C application has been lodged. The timing of any withdrawal should be planned with your advisor, so that it does not affect the application.

Is English required?

English

  • No. Good English helps with points and avoids the second instalment visa application charge, which applies to applicants aged 18 and over. The amount follows the Department of Home Affairs (DoHA) fee schedule current at the time of lodgement.

Do children go to school for free?

Education

  • Yes. Once the 188 visa is granted, children can attend public school from primary through senior secondary, under the policy that applies to permanent residents and eligible visa holders; the exact conditions are set by each state.

How does Medicare work on the 188 and on the 888?

Medicare

  • At the 188 (provisional) stage there is normally no Medicare access, so private health cover is needed. Once the 888C (PR) is granted, the family is eligible for Medicare.

Up to what age can children be included?

Dependents

  • Children under 23 who are still dependent can be included in the application, with age locked at the time the 188 visa is granted. It is worth checking each child's age early so that no one ages out.

What is the path to Australian citizenship?

Australian citizenship

  • The citizenship criteria are assessed separately from PR: generally 4 years of lawful residence in Australia, of which at least 1 year as a permanent resident; no more than 90 days outside Australia in the final year; passing the citizenship test; good character; and meeting the language requirement.

Do connections speed up a decision?

How applications are decided

  • There is no such thing as using "connections" to have an application prioritized or the requirements relaxed — that would be against Australian law. Applications are decided on the data and the evidence.
