- Muc dich trang: trang dich vu gioi thieu dien 188C / SIV. Chuong trinh DA DONG voi ho so moi tu 31/07/2024 (BIIP closed permanently). Nguoi doc thuc te: nguoi dang giu 188C chuan bi len 888C, hoac nguoi tra cuu xem con mo khong.

- Thi truong hop: AU, SG, HK, UAE, UK — tim bang "Significant Investor Visa" hoac "SIV" hoac ma 188C.
- Giong: co van diem tinh kieu private bank, ngoi THU BA ("the investor", "188C visa holders"). KHONG dung "you/your".
