[s003] What is the 188C visa?
[s004] The 188C Significant Investor visa was the Significant Investor stream of Australia's Business Innovation and Investment Program, for investors with substantial financial capacity.
[s005] Investors placed A$5,000,000 in complying investments in Australia.
[s006] The investment has to be held as required for the investor to be considered for 888C permanent residence.
[s007] A spouse or partner and children under 23 could be included in the same application.
[s008|#bo-qua-so: ngày 31/07/2024 viết theo lối Mỹ "July 31, 2024" nên bản Anh không còn số 7 đứng riêng] The program closed permanently to new applications on July 31, 2024. This page is a reference for applications already lodged and for the 888C stage that follows.

[s010] Benefits of the 188C visa
[s011] The whole family moves together
[s012] A spouse or partner and children under 23 are granted visas alongside the main applicant.
[s013] Living in Australia
[s014] The whole family can live, study, work, and run a business in Australia while the visa is valid.
[s015] Schooling in Australia
[s016] Children can attend public school under the policy of each state or territory.
[s017] Flexible travel
[s018] Multiple entries and exits between Australia and the holder's home country while the visa is valid.
[s019] A pathway to 888C permanent residence
[s020] Holders can apply for 888C permanent residence once the investment and residence requirements are met.
[s021] Advantages of the 188C visa
[s022] No age limit
[s023] The 188C set no maximum age for the applicant.
[s024|#bo-qua-tn: dòng chốt «thang điểm di trú» = «selection factor grid» là tên của New Brunswick (Canada); Úc gọi là points test — xem dòng «thang điểm di trú Úc»] No points test
[s025] There was no points score to reach, unlike the 188A and 188B streams.
[s026] No business to run
[s027] Participation is through fund investments rather than establishing or running a business in Australia.
[s028] Passive investment through funds
[s029] The investment may produce distributions depending on how the funds perform, with no fixed rate of return promised.
[s030] A light physical residence requirement
[s031] For the 888C stage, the main applicant had to spend at least 40 days a year in Australia, or the accompanying spouse or partner at least 180 days a year.
[s032] Who could apply for the 188C visa
[s033] Applicant requirements
[s034] Invest A$5,000,000 in complying investments in Australia.
[s035] Evidence of a lawful net worth of at least A$5,000,000.
[s036] Nomination by an Australian state or territory.
[s037] Clear security, background and medical checks.
[s038] How the investment had to be allocated
[s039] At least A$1,000,000 in venture capital and growth private equity funds.
[s040] At least A$1,500,000 in emerging companies funds.
[s041] At least A$2,500,000 in a balancing investment.
[s042] Dependents who could be included
[s043] A spouse or de facto partner, with evidence of the relationship.
[s044] Children under 23 who are unmarried and financially dependent.
[s045] The 188C application process
[s046] Assessment and case preparation
[s047] Review of the applicant's profile, assets, source of capital, and ability to meet the program's requirements.
[s048] Designing the investment portfolio
[s049] Choosing a suitable investment structure with fund managers and financial advisers.
[s050] Expression of Interest and state or territory nomination
[s051] Lodging an Expression of Interest (EOI) and applying for nomination from an Australian state or territory.
[s052] Lodging the 188C application
[s053] Once nomination was granted, the visa application was lodged with the Department of Home Affairs.
[s054] Making the investment when required
[s055] Transferring the investment into complying funds and completing the health requirements.
[s056] Visa grant and ongoing conditions
[s057] After the visa is granted, the investor moves to Australia, holds the investment, and meets the residence conditions for the 888C stage.

[s079] 188C visa FAQs
[s080] Is the 188C visa still open to new applications?
[s081] Program status
[s082|#bo-qua-so: ngày 31/07/2024 viết theo lối Mỹ "July 31, 2024" nên bản Anh không còn số 7 đứng riêng] No. The 188C Significant Investor stream closed permanently to new applications on July 31, 2024. Applications lodged before that date continue to be processed under the rules in force.
[s083] Did the 188C require running a business in Australia?
[s084] How the investment works
[s085] No. The 188C was an investment stream using complying funds, and it did not require the investor to establish or run a business.
[s086] Was proof of source of funds required?
[s087] Source of funds
[s088] Yes. Investors had to show that the A$5,000,000 investment came from a lawful source of funds.
[s089] Is the investment at risk?
[s090] Investment risk
[s091] Yes. Investing through funds puts capital at risk. Returns and the prospect of recovering the capital depend on how the funds perform and on the terms of the investment.
[s092] Does a loss in the funds affect the 888C application?
[s093] Effect on the 888C stage
[s094] The core condition is that the complying investment is held as required. Even so, the outcome depends on the individual case and on the decision of the immigration authorities.
[s095] Was English required?
[s096] English
[s097] No level of English was required. Where functional English was not shown, the applicant and family members aged 18 and over could have to pay the second instalment of the visa application charge in force at the time of lodgement.
[s098] Does the 188C visa include Medicare?
[s099] Medicare
[s100] During the 188C provisional stage, the family needs private health cover. Medicare applies once permanent residence eligibility is reached.
