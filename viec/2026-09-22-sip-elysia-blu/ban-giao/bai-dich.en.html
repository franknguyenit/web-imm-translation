Cyprus real estate can be more than an income-producing asset held in euros. Owning at Elysia Blu also opens a route to permanent residence in Cyprus for your whole family.

Your route to permanent residence in Cyprus

A qualifying real estate purchase

A qualifying property starts at €300,000 plus VAT and must be bought new from the developer.

Prepare the file

The application can include you, your spouse, and your dependent children. Besides owning the property, you need to show a secured annual income.

Become a permanent resident

You need to visit Cyprus once every 2 years to keep the status.

Cyprus permanent residence is the right to live in Cyprus; it is not Cypriot citizenship, and it does not give the right to live or work across the whole EU.

Living in Cyprus

  • You and your family can live, study, and do business in Cyprus with the rights of a permanent resident.
  • Access to a European standard of living, a Mediterranean climate, and an international community.
  • Visa applications to other European countries can be more straightforward, depending on each country's rules.

Healthcare, education, and business

  • A healthcare system to European standards, subject to the conditions of the General Healthcare System (GESY).
  • Education on the British model, which suits a long-term education plan for your children.
  • Corporate tax of 15% from 2026, among the more competitive rates in Europe.
  • Personal tax depends on your tax residence status, your sources of income, and the rules in force.

Points to note about permanent residence

  • Cyprus permanent residence is not Cypriot citizenship.
  • The right to live and work in other EU countries arises only if you hold EU citizenship or a valid residence or work permit in that country.
  • Your right to work and do business, and the tax treatment that applies, must be assessed against the program conditions and each investor's own file.

The route to Cypriot citizenship

Hold permanent residence (PR)

The physical permanent residence card is renewed every 5 years, provided you still own the property.

Meet the residence and language conditions

At least 8 years of physical residence within an 11-year period, Greek language at CEFR level B1, and a pass in the cultural integration test.

Cypriot (EU) citizenship

Dual citizenship is allowed. A Cypriot passport gives visa-free travel to more than 170 destinations.
