2 investment routes

  • Standard route: NZ$1 million, held for 3 years
  • Fast route: NZ$2 million, held for 12 months
  • Purpose: temporary residence leading to New Zealand permanent residence
  • INZ has accepted applications since November 2025

How you invest

  • Buy a New Zealand business outright, or hold at least 25% of its shares
  • Standard route: NZ$1 million; after 3 years running the business, you apply for a Resident Visa
  • Fast route: NZ$2 million; after 12 months running the business, you apply for a Resident Visa

Job creation requirements

  • Maintain 5 full-time jobs
  • Create at least 1 new job for a New Zealand citizen or permanent resident

Key benefits

The whole family can live, study, and work in New Zealand

Free public healthcare and free public schooling for your children through the end of secondary school

Permanent residence after 12 months on the fast route, or 3 years on the standard route

After 5 years of residence, you can apply for New Zealand citizenship

The New Zealand passport — visa-free or eVisa access to 180+ countries

Strengths and limitations

The fast route reaches permanent residence in just 12 months

A reasonable entry level, from NZ$1 million — far below Active Investor Plus

Full control over running the business and the investment; it suits people who want to operate a real business

You have to run the business yourself — it does not suit passive investment

How the business performs affects the permanent residence decision

An age limit and a demanding physical presence requirement
