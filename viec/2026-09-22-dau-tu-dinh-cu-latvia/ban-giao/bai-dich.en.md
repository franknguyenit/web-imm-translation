# Latvia Golden Visa

### What is the Latvia residence by investment program?

- The Latvia Golden Visa lets you and your family obtain a Latvian temporary residence permit through a qualifying investment.
- With a Latvian residence permit, you and your family can travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules.
- You can choose between a real estate investment and a share capital investment in a Latvian company.
- **Indicative processing time:** 30–90 days, depending on your file and on the authorities' own timelines.
- After 5 years, if you qualify, you can be considered for permanent residence, and after 10 years for Latvian citizenship, provided you meet the conditions.

## Latvia Golden Visa requirements

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older.
- Meets background security and medical requirements.
- You document a lawful source of funds for the investment.

### Requirements for dependents

- Dependents joining the application must meet the background security and medical requirements.
- Family applications are assessed under the rules in force when you file.

### The real estate route

- Purchase real estate in Riga valued at €250,000.
- Hold the investment for at least 5 years.

### The share capital route

- Invest at least €50,000 in the share capital of a Latvian company with up to 50 employees.
- Or invest at least €100,000 in a company with more than 50 employees.
- The company must pay at least €10,000 a year in corporate income tax.

## Latvia residence by investment FAQs

#### Is the Latvian residence permit the same as permanent residence?

The Latvian temporary residence permit

- No. It is a temporary residence permit. You can be considered for permanent residence after 5 years if you meet the conditions.

#### Can I travel in the Schengen Area?

Travel in the Schengen Area

- Yes. The Latvian residence permit allows travel within the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules.

#### Do I have to live in Latvia continuously?

Residence conditions

- You are not required to live in Latvia continuously to keep the temporary residence permit, but you do need to meet the applicable renewal conditions.

#### Can I work in Latvia?

The right to work

- Yes. You and your family can work in Latvia while you hold valid residence status.

#### Can I obtain Latvian citizenship?

A route to citizenship

- It is possible. After 10 years, and once you meet the conditions set by Latvian law, you can be considered for citizenship.

#### What are the main risks?

The main risks

- The risks usually lie in choosing an unsuitable property or business, in a source of funds file that is not clear enough, or in failing to keep the investment conditions in place.
