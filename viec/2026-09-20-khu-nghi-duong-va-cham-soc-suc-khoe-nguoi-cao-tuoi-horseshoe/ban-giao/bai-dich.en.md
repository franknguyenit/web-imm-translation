## Senior living and care community

# Horseshoe Bay Senior Living

- A rural EB-5 project: a Class A senior living community in Horseshoe Bay, Texas, a well-known resort town.
- According to the project sponsor, it is the only new, modern senior living development in an area facing a severe supply shortage.
- Located in a rural Targeted Employment Area (rural TEA), which qualifies Form I-526E petitions for priority processing by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS).
- The EB-5 capital is structured as a senior loan secured by the project itself.
- EB-5 capital is raised and managed by EB5 Capital, an experienced regional center.

## A senior living and care community in Horseshoe Bay, Texas

- The senior living community offers a full continuum of care, including:

- 114 Independent Living units - 48 Assisted Living units - 18 Memory Care units

## Meeting unmet demand for senior living

#### Demographic tailwinds

- As baby boomers reach the age at which they need more care, the U.S. senior housing sector is positioned to benefit.
- The population aged 75 and older is projected to grow 3 times faster than the overall population.

#### A severe supply shortage

- There are only 5 senior care facilities within a 10-mile radius, all operating at more than 90% occupancy.
- The area faces a shortfall of nearly 1,000 beds, far more than the project's 182 beds.

#### Positioned in the Class A segment

- No facility in the area currently meets Class A standards.
- Existing facilities were built before 2000, and even the newest has been operating since 2014.
- No other projects are currently under construction or in planning.
- Once complete, the project is expected to be the only premium, modern option in the area.

#### A continuum-of-care model that helps retain residents

- The project offers all 3 levels of care: Independent Living, Assisted Living, and Memory Care.
- As residents' health declines and they need a higher level of care, they can transition within the same community without having to move out.
- This supports longer resident stays and more stable cash flow while reducing resident acquisition costs.

#### Families want their parents close by

- Adult children who live in the area want to move their parents nearby so they can visit more easily.
- The project is expected to attract residents from across the country to the area.
- It offers older adults a comfortable, Class A care setting, and gives children and grandchildren an ideal place to visit and vacation.

#### An experienced development and operating team

- **Falcon Senior Housing:** a professional developer with a real estate portfolio valued at more than US$1.5 billion.
- **SilverPoint Senior Living:** currently directly manages 9 senior living communities with nearly 700 units.

## Priority processing and set-aside visas

#### Rural TEA: priority processing by USCIS

- The project is located in a rural TEA as defined under the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022 (RIA), enacted in March 2022.
- Under current rules, USCIS gives priority processing to Form I-526E petitions for rural projects.
- Approval is anticipated in under 12 months, although actual processing times may vary.

#### Set-aside visas for rural projects

- After Form I-526 approval, investors can draw on the 20% of EB-5 visas set aside for rural projects.
- This may shorten the current wait for a visa.

## Structural features designed to manage EB-5 investment risk

#### Collateral securing the EB-5 loan

- A first-position lien on the entire site and all improvements on it.
- A first-position lien on the project's operating cash flow.

#### Collateral value relative to debt

- Stabilized project value: US$142.2 million

- A substantial projected cushion between asset value and total debt, supporting the ability to refinance if needed.
