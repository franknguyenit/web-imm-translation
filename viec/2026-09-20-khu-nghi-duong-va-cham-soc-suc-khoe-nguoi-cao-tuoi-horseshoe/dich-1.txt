[s038] Demographic tailwinds
[s039] As baby boomers reach the age when they need more health care, the U.S. senior housing sector stands to benefit.
[s040] The population aged 75 and older is projected to grow 3 times faster than the overall population.
[s041] A severe supply shortage
[s042] There are only 5 senior care facilities within a 10-mile radius, all operating at more than 90% occupancy.
[s043] The area is short nearly 1,000 beds, far more than the project's 182 beds.
[s044] Positioned in the Class A segment
[s045] No facility in the area currently meets Class A standards.
[s046] Existing facilities were built before 2000, and even the newest has been operating since 2014.
[s047] No other projects are currently under construction or in planning.
[s048] Once complete, the project will be the only premium, modern option in the area.
[s049] A continuum-of-care model that helps retain residents
[s050] The project offers all 3 levels of care: Independent Living, Assisted Living, and Memory Care.
[s051] As residents' health declines and they need a higher level of care, they can move to a different part of the community without having to leave.
[s052] This supports long resident stays and stable cash flow, and reduces the cost of attracting new residents.
[s053] Families want their parents close by
[s054] Adult children who live in the area want to move their parents nearby so they can visit more easily.
[s055] The project draws residents from across the country to the area.
[s056] It offers older adults a comfortable, Class A care setting, and gives children and grandchildren an ideal place to visit and vacation.

[s060] Priority processing and set-aside visas
[s061] Rural TEA: priority processing by USCIS
[s062] The project is located in a rural TEA as defined under the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022 (RIA), enacted in March 2022.
[s063] Form I-526E petitions receive priority processing from USCIS under current rules.
[s064] Approval is anticipated in under 12 months, although actual processing times may vary.
[s065] Set-aside visas for rural projects
[s066] After Form I-526 approval, investors can draw on the 20% of EB-5 visas set aside for rural projects.
[s067] This may shorten the current wait for a visa.
