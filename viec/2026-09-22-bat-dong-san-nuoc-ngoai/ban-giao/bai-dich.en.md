Why international real estate

Property abroad can produce regular income in a hard currency while acting as a store of value and spreading risk through portfolio diversification. Beyond the financial side, owning an asset in a market with clear property law — together with easier travel, the right of residence, or a fallback plan for the family — creates long-term value that goes beyond investment return alone.

## Two ways to own and invest in international real estate

Depending on your financial objective and your plans for the family, you can follow either of the two routes most investors take today.

Route 1

### Real estate with residence rights

Buy a property that meets the program's investment threshold to obtain a residence permit or permanent residence for you and your family, while keeping full ownership of the asset.

- check_circleFull ownership of the property, with the right to resell after the holding period
- check_circleResidence rights for your spouse, children, and parents (depending on the program)
- check_circleTravel in the Schengen area under the European Golden Visa programs

Investment threshold

From €250,000

See the projectsarrow_forward

Route 2

### Real estate with citizenship

Invest in a government-approved real estate project to obtain a second passport; processing takes months rather than years, and most programs have no residence requirement.

- check_circleA second citizenship for the whole family, passed on to the next generation
- check_circleProcessed in 4–8 months, with no residence or language requirement
- check_circleThe right to resell the property or the project share after the required period

Investment threshold

From US$400,000

## The two investment routes side by side

Setting out the legal basis, the type of asset, and the countries involved helps you and your family choose the route that fits your long-term objective.

shieldResidence rights

languageSecond citizenship

Route

Real estate with residence rights (Golden Visa / permanent residence)

You own a qualifying property and receive a residence permit for the whole family.

Real estate with citizenship

You invest in a government-approved project and are granted citizenship and a passport.

Goal

check_circleAdds a lawful right of residence in the destination country

- A residence permit or permanent residence (PR) for the whole family
- Travel in the Schengen area with a European Golden Visa, and wider international travel
- A base for long-term study and living in the host country

check_circleA second citizenship and passport

- Full citizenship rights, and wider international travel
- A global citizenship fallback plan for the whole family
- No minimum physical residence in the country you invest in

The asset

100% freehold ownership of the property

The title deed is in your own name — a premium apartment, a seafront villa, or a qualifying restoration or change-of-use property.

Property ownership, or a share in a flagship project

100% ownership of an apartment (Türkiye), or a share in the project with a projected — not guaranteed — annual return (Dominica, Grenada).
