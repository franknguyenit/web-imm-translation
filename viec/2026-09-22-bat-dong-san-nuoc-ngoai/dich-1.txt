[s021] Why international real estate
[s022] Property abroad can produce steady income in a hard currency while acting as a store of value and spreading risk, on the usual principle of portfolio diversification. Beyond the financial side, owning an asset in a market with clear property law — together with easier travel, the right of residence, or a fallback plan for the family — creates long-term value that goes beyond investment return alone.
