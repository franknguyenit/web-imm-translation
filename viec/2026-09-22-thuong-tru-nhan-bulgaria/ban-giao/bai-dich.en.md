# Bulgaria Golden Visa

### What is the Bulgaria Golden Visa?

- The Bulgaria Golden Visa is a Bulgarian government program that grants permanent residence to non-EU investors and their families.
- You invest in a fund licensed by the Bulgarian Financial Supervision Commission (FSC).
- Residence in Bulgaria with no time limit — a full member of the Schengen Area since January 2025.
- A minimum investment of BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300 at the fixed rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583), held for at least 5 years.
- Taxes, fees, and related costs are not included.

## Benefits of Bulgarian permanent residence

### Permanent residence for the whole family

- You, your spouse, and your dependent children all receive permanent residence cards.

### Live, study, and work with no time limit

- In an EU country where the cost of living is moderate.

### Visa-free travel in the Schengen Area

- Bulgaria has been a full Schengen member since January 2025; you can travel across the 29-country area, spending up to 90 days in any 180-day period outside Bulgaria.

### 10% personal income tax

- A flat rate, among the lowest in the EU.

### A route to citizenship and the US E-2 visa

- Once you meet the residence, language, and integration requirements set by law, you may be eligible to apply for naturalization. Bulgaria is one of the treaty countries whose citizens can apply for the US E-2 treaty investor visa.

## Advantages of the Bulgaria Golden Visa

### A fund investment only

- No donation to the government is required.

### No physical residence requirement

- For the first 5 years you are not required to live in Bulgaria; after 5 years, you need only visit an EU country once a year and Bulgaria once every 6 years to keep the card.

### You can withdraw your capital after 5 years

- You may withdraw the investment once the program's minimum 5-year holding period has passed, without affecting your permanent residence.

### Permanent residence from the start

- There is no temporary residence stage, and the program can open a path to Bulgarian — and therefore EU — citizenship once you meet the residence and language requirements.

### Straightforward eligibility

- No interview, and no language, education, or business experience requirements.

### No age limit for applicants

- Aged 18 or older, with no upper age limit.

## Requirements

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older, with no upper age limit.

### Meets security and background vetting requirements

- You document a lawful source of funds for the investment

### Dependents you can include

- Your legal spouse, where the marriage has lasted at least 5 years

### Children under 18

- Meets security and background vetting requirements

### How you invest

- An investment of at least BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300 at the fixed rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583) in a qualifying fund — a collective investment scheme or an alternative investment fund (AIF) — held for at least 5 years.
- The fund must be licensed by the Bulgarian Financial Supervision Commission (FSC).
- Size thresholds: a collective investment scheme must have a net asset value (NAV) of at least BGN 5,000,000; an AIF must have assets of at least BGN 3,000,000.
- The investment strategy focuses on shares and bonds of listed Bulgarian companies.
- Note: this is an at-risk investment, and the value of the fund moves with the market.

## The process

### Deposit and due diligence

Sign the deposit agreement and complete the initial due diligence on your file.

### Sign the contracts

Complete the service agreement for your Bulgaria Golden Visa application.

### File for pre-approval

Prepare and file your application for pre-approval from the InvestBulgaria Agency (IBA).

### Invest and receive your certificate

Once the IBA approves your file, you invest at least BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300) in the fund and submit proof of payment; the IBA then issues your investment certificate.

### Apply for a Type D visa

Apply for the Type D long-stay visa that allows you to enter Bulgaria.

### File for your residence card

Travel to Bulgaria to file your permanent residence application.

### Collect your residence card

Once approved, you travel to Bulgaria a second time to collect your permanent residence card.

## Bulgaria Golden Visa FAQs

#### What is the Bulgaria Golden Visa, and does it grant permanent residence?

What the Bulgaria Golden Visa is, and whether it grants permanent residence

- The Bulgaria Golden Visa grants permanent residence to non-EU investors and their families through an investment in an FSC-licensed fund. The whole family can live, study, and work in Bulgaria with no time limit and travel in the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules. It can also be a first step toward naturalization if you meet the residence, language, and integration requirements set by law.

#### Is a fund investment risky?

Whether a fund investment carries risk

- Yes. A fund investment carries risk: the value of the fund moves with the market, and neither returns nor the recovery of your capital is guaranteed. A qualifying fund has to be licensed and supervised by the Bulgarian FSC. IMM Group reviews the fund and your source of funds in detail before any contract is signed, to help manage that risk. Read the fund's offering documents carefully before you decide.

#### Can I withdraw my capital, and when?

Whether and when you can withdraw your capital

- Under the program rules you must hold the investment for at least 5 years. After that, you may withdraw your capital and keep your permanent residence. The exact timing and conditions depend on the type of fund and its offering documents — contact IMM Group for advice on your own case.

#### How many days a year do I have to spend in Bulgaria?

Days you have to spend in Bulgaria each year

- For the first 5 years the program does not require you to live in Bulgaria. After 5 years, you need only visit an EU country once a year and Bulgaria once every 6 years to keep the card. If you are aiming at naturalization later, you will need to meet the residence and integration requirements set by Bulgarian law.

#### Is Bulgaria in the Schengen Area, and does it use the euro?

Bulgaria's Schengen membership and currency

- Yes to both. Bulgaria became a full member of the Schengen Area on January 1, 2025, when land border controls were lifted, and adopted the euro on January 1, 2026, at the fixed conversion rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583. Both changes make travel and banking in Bulgaria simpler for investors.

#### Who can be included in the application?

Family members you can include

- The application can include your legal spouse, where the marriage has lasted at least 5 years, and children under 18. Dependents must meet the security and background vetting requirements. Every family is different — contact IMM Group for advice on who can be included and what documents you will need.

#### Is documenting my source of funds complicated?

Is documenting my source of funds complicated?

- You need to show that your investment capital comes from a lawful source. The requirements are generally lighter than for some of the US, Australian, and Canadian programs, but every family documents this differently — a business, the sale of a property, savings built up over time. IMM Group reviews your position and advises on the way to document your source of funds for your particular file.
