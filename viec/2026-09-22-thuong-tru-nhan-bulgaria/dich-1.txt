[s043] How you invest
[s044] From BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300 at the fixed rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583) into a qualifying fund — a collective investment scheme or an alternative investment fund (AIF) — held for at least 5 years.
[s045] The fund must be licensed by the Bulgarian Financial Supervision Commission (FSC).
[s046] Size thresholds: a collective investment scheme must hold net assets of at least BGN 5,000,000; an AIF must hold assets of at least BGN 3,000,000.
[s047] The investment strategy focuses on shares and bonds of listed Bulgarian companies.
[s048] Note: this is an investment that carries risk, and the value of the fund moves with the market.

[s065] Bulgaria Golden Visa FAQs
[s066] What is the Bulgaria Golden Visa, and does it grant permanent residence?
[s067] What the Bulgaria Golden Visa is, and whether it grants permanent residence
[s068] The Bulgaria Golden Visa grants permanent residence to non-EU investors and their families through an investment in an FSC-licensed fund. The whole family can live, study, and work in Bulgaria with no time limit and travel in the Schengen Area under the short-stay rules. It can also be a first step toward naturalization if you meet the residence, language, and integration requirements set by law.
[s069] Is a fund investment risky?
[s070] Whether a fund investment carries risk
[s071] Yes. A fund investment carries risk: the value of the fund moves with the market, and neither returns nor the recovery of your capital is guaranteed. A qualifying fund has to be licensed and supervised by the Bulgarian FSC. IMM Group reviews the fund and your source of funds in detail before any contract is signed, to limit that risk. Read the fund's offering documents carefully before you decide.
[s072] Can I withdraw my capital, and when?
[s073] Whether and when you can withdraw your capital
[s074] Under the program rules you must hold the investment for at least 5 years. After that, you may withdraw your capital and keep your permanent residence. The exact timing and conditions depend on the type of fund and its offering documents — contact IMM Group for advice on your own case.
[s075] How many days a year do I have to spend in Bulgaria?
[s076] Days you have to spend in Bulgaria each year
[s077] For the first 5 years the program does not require you to live in Bulgaria. After 5 years, you need only visit an EU country once a year and Bulgaria once every 6 years to keep the card. If you are aiming at naturalization later, you will need to meet the residence and integration requirements set by Bulgarian law.
[s078] Is Bulgaria in the Schengen Area, and does it use the euro?
[s079] Bulgaria's Schengen membership and currency
[s080] Yes to both. Bulgaria became a full member of the Schengen Area on January 1, 2025, when land border controls were lifted, and adopted the euro on January 1, 2026 at the fixed rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583. Both changes make travel and banking in Bulgaria simpler for investors.
[s081] Who can be included in the application?
[s082] Family members you can include
[s083] The application can include your legal spouse, where the marriage has lasted at least 5 years, and children under 18. Dependents must meet the security and background vetting requirements. Every family is different — contact IMM Group for advice on who can be included and what documents you will need.
[s084] Is documenting my source of funds complicated?
[s085] Is documenting my source of funds complicated?
[s086] You need to show that your investment capital comes from a lawful source. The requirements are generally lighter than for some of the US, Australian, and Canadian programs, but every family documents this differently — a business, the sale of a property, savings built up over time. IMM Group reviews your position and advises on the way to document your source of funds for your particular file.
