[s004] The Bulgaria Golden Visa is a Bulgarian government program that grants permanent residence to non-EU investors and their families.
[s006] Residence in Bulgaria with no time limit — a full member of the Schengen Area since January 2025.
[s007] A minimum investment of BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300 at the fixed rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583), held for at least 5 years.
[s012] You, your spouse, and your dependent children all receive permanent residence cards.
[s016] Bulgaria has been a full Schengen member since January 2025; you can travel across the 29-country area, spending up to 90 days in any 180-day period outside Bulgaria.
[s020] Once you meet the residence, language, and integration requirements set by law, you may be eligible to apply for naturalization. Bulgaria is one of the treaty countries whose citizens can apply for the US E-2 treaty investor visa.
[s025] For the first 5 years you are not required to live in Bulgaria; after that, you need only visit an EU country once a year and Bulgaria once every 6 years to keep the card.
[s026] You can withdraw your capital after 5 years
[s027] You may withdraw the investment once the program's minimum 5-year holding period has passed, without affecting your permanent residence.
[s029] There is no temporary residence stage, and the program can open a path to Bulgarian — and therefore EU — citizenship once you meet the residence and language requirements.

[s044] An investment of at least BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300 at the fixed rate of €1 = BGN 1.95583) in a qualifying fund — a collective investment scheme or an alternative investment fund (AIF) — held for at least 5 years.
[s046] Size thresholds: a collective investment scheme must have a net asset value (NAV) of at least BGN 5,000,000; an AIF must have assets of at least BGN 3,000,000.
[s048] Note: this is an at-risk investment, and the value of the fund moves with the market.
[s057] Once the IBA approves your file, you invest at least BGN 1,000,000 (about €511,300) in the fund and submit proof of payment; the IBA then issues your investment certificate.
