A light physical residence requirement

Ireland golden visa requirements

Applicant requirements

  • Aged 18 or older, with no upper age limit
  • Meets security and background vetting requirements
  • Minimum net worth of €2 million

Your spouse and dependent children

  • Children under 24 who are unmarried, in full-time education, and financially dependent
  • Meets security and background vetting requirements

Investment requirements

  • Evidence that the investment capital comes from a lawful source.

2 investment options

  • Option 1: Invest €1 million (approximately US$1.15 million) in a qualifying enterprise or project, and hold the investment for 3 years.
  • Option 2: Make an endowment of €400,000 (approximately US$459,000) to a social project in the arts, culture, sport or education.

Please note: the Ireland IIP stopped accepting new applications on February 15, 2023. IMM Group holds a limited number of eligible endowment places from projects that met the legal criteria before that date.

Frequently asked questions

Can you buy real estate to get Irish residency?

No — the Ireland IIP has no real estate purchase option.

There are only 2 options:

  • An endowment of €400,000 to a social project
  • An investment of €1 million in an enterprise — held for 3 years

Which option do most investors choose?

  • In practice, most of our clients have chosen the endowment option
  • IMM Group was the first firm to process Ireland applications in Vietnam (since 2017).

Three reasons clients choose the endowment option

  • Lowest cost — €400,000
  • Simpler process — no need to maintain the investment for 3 years
  • Aligns with the timeline for children to relocate and study with free tuition

What is a Stamp 4 visa in Ireland?

Stamp 4 — long-term residence status under the IIP

  • Stamp 4 is a form of residence permission (not a visa); the physical card is the Irish Residence Permit (IRP)
  • Issued after the application is approved by ISD in Ireland — processing time depends on ISD schedules
  • Allows you to live, work, do business, and study in Ireland under Stamp 4 conditions
  • Has a fixed validity period and must be renewed as required
  • Once you meet the reckonable residence requirements, you may apply for naturalization

Does Stamp 4 allow free travel in the Schengen Area and the EU?

  • Stamp 4 does not yet provide visa-free access to the Schengen Area
  • Travel to the Schengen Area still requires a travel visa
  • Holding Irish residence status may make the Schengen visa process more straightforward
  • After naturalizing as an Irish citizen: eligible to travel, live, work, and study in EU countries, subject to the applicable rules.

What is the difference between the Schengen Area and the EU?

The Schengen Area is not the same thing as the EU

  • EU — the European Union: 27 countries, an intergovernmental organization
  • Schengen: currently 29 countries that allow free movement
  • Ireland is an EU member but is not part of the Schengen Area
  • Stamp 4 holders still need a Schengen visa for travel.

Can your children study in other EU countries?

  • No — with Stamp 4, children may only study in Ireland
  • To study in another EU country: a student visa is required
  • To study freely across the EU: Irish citizenship is required (once you are eligible)

Do you pay Irish tax on income earned outside Ireland?

  • Tax obligations depend on tax residency and the source of the income
  • Under Irish rules, an individual is treated as a tax resident once they are present for the required time thresholds (generally more than 183 days per year, or under a cumulative formula)
  • Short stays in Ireland (for example, 1 day per year) generally do not meet the tax residency thresholds
  • We recommend consulting an independent tax advisor before proceeding

Stamp 4 validity and renewal

Stamp 4 — renewal timeline

  • First issued for: 2 years
  • First renewal: an additional 3 years
  • Subsequent renewals: 5 years each

Renewal conditions

  • Enterprise investment option: maintain the investment as required
  • Endowment option: file a renewal application in line with ISD requirements

Can you withdraw the investment after 3 years?

  • You may withdraw the capital after 3 years — on its own, this does not affect your renewal, provided the other conditions are met
  • The only requirement is to maintain the investment for 3 years
  • Governed by the rules of Immigration Service Delivery (ISD) in Ireland
  • Renewal conditions after 3 years
  • Meet the residence requirement of 1 day per year
  • No breaches of the law inside or outside Ireland

How long until you can apply for Irish citizenship?

You may apply for naturalization once you meet the reckonable residence requirements (a minimum of approximately 5 years under the rules)

  • You can meet the residence requirement in 1 of 2 ways:

Option 1 — continuous residence

  • Absences from Ireland of no more than 6 weeks per year during the first 4 years
  • Continuous residence for the 12 months of year 5, immediately before you apply

Option 2 — cumulative residence within a 9-year window

  • For example, applying in year 8: accumulate a total of 4 years within the first 6 years
  • Continuous residence for 12 months in year 7

Note: the outcome of a naturalization application depends on approval by the competent Irish authorities

What does Irish citizenship give you?

Benefits after naturalizing as an Irish citizen

  • Full rights of an Irish citizen under Irish law
  • Eligible to live in the 27 EU member states
  • Eligible to live, study, and work in the UK through the Common Travel Area (CTA), subject to the applicable rules
  • Eligible to apply for British citizenship once the UK residence conditions are met — with an exemption from the English language test
  • The Irish passport ranks among the world's strongest — supporting visa-free or eVisa travel to many countries

Do I have to pass an Irish language test to obtain citizenship?

  • No language test is required — not even in English
  • You only need to meet the residence conditions and file the application
  • This differs from Portugal and Greece, which require a test in the national language

What are the conditions for British citizenship once I hold Irish citizenship?

Conditions considered for British citizenship

  • Aged over 18
  • 5 years of residence in the UK before the date of application
  • Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) for the final 12 months
  • English at B1 level (Irish citizens are exempt)
  • Passing the Life in the UK test
  • Demonstrating an intention to live in the UK long term
  • Meets security and background vetting requirements

Note on time spent outside the UK

  • No more than 450 days outside the UK over the 5 years
  • No more than 90 days outside the UK in the final 12 months

Can I hold 3 citizenships — Irish, British, and my existing nationality?

  • You may hold more than one citizenship — this must be assessed under the nationality law of your home country
  • Ireland and the UK both allow dual or multiple citizenship
  • For your home country: holding an additional foreign citizenship must be assessed under your home country's nationality law — we recommend consulting an independent legal advisor

Do I need British citizenship if I already have Irish citizenship?

  • Not necessarily — Irish citizenship already opens the right to live in the UK
  • Irish citizenship allows you to live in the UK under the Common Travel Area (CTA)
  • Under the CTA, Irish citizens are not treated as "foreign nationals" in the UK
  • If you still wish to naturalize as a British citizen, you are exempt from the language test.

What are the investment risks?

Three main risks of the Ireland Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP)

  • Enterprise investment option: choosing an unreliable project or developer, or a project that is not viable
  • Both options: an unclear source of funds leading to refusal of the application
  • An attorney or immigration firm without experience in proving the source of funds

Is the Ireland program stable?

The program has a long operating track record in Europe, although it no longer accepts new applications

  • One of the EU's first residency-by-investment programs, alongside Portugal
  • Launched in the aftermath of the 2007–2008 financial crisis
  • Well regarded in the region for its maturity and transparency
  • Processing times depend on ISD schedules in Ireland

Who can be included in the application?

Family members who can be included in the application

  • Your legal spouse
  • Children under 24 who are unmarried, in full-time education, and financially dependent
  • The dependent-child policy is relatively generous compared with many other programs.

Is dependency straightforward to prove?

Proving dependency is straightforward

  • Only your children's birth certificates are needed
  • Children under 18: no proof of dependency required
  • Children aged 18–24: evidence that they are still in education and financially dependent

Are adopted children and stepchildren eligible?

Adopted children and stepchildren are eligible

  • Adopted child: a legal certificate is required
  • Stepchild: a legal certificate plus a notarized letter of consent from the spouse
  • IMM Group assists with preparing the documents.

Can parents be included in the application?

  • No — the program does not accept parents within the same application
  • An application covers only the main applicant, the spouse, and dependent children
  • IMM Group assists with visitor visa applications for parents
  • Alternatives if you want to bring the whole family
  • Move to the Portugal Golden Visa
  • File a separate Ireland application for the parents

Is there a language requirement?

  • No language requirement — and no age or management experience requirement
  • Basic conditions
  • Personal net worth of at least €2 million from a lawful source
  • Choose 1 of the 2 investment options
  • Cash requirement by option
  • Endowment option: €400,000 held as cash or in a savings account
  • Enterprise investment option: €1 million in cash ready to invest

Is there an annual application quota?

  • Note: the Ireland IIP stopped accepting new applications on February 15, 2023
  • Previously, the program opened application windows according to the ISD calendar during the year
  • Only investment places in projects that were legally eligible before that date are still being accepted, in order to complete their target funding

How many days a year do I have to spend in Ireland?

  • Just 1 day per year to maintain Stamp 4
  • Note: this applies only to maintaining IIP residency — it is not the requirement for citizenship
  • Tip: travel to Ireland late in 1 year and stay into the next — a single trip can cover the requirement for 2 years.
