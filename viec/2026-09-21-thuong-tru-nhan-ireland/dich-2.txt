[s047] Ireland golden visa requirements
[s048] Applicant requirements
[s049] Aged 18 or older, with no upper age limit
[s050] Meets security and background vetting requirements
[s051|#bo-qua-so: số VND chỉ nhắc lại số EUR] Minimum net worth of €2 million
[s052] Dependents you can include
[s053] Children under 24 who are unmarried, in full-time education and financially dependent
[s055] Investment requirements
[s056] Evidence that the investment capital comes from a lawful source.
[s057] Two investment options
[s058|#bo-qua-so: số VND chỉ nhắc lại số EUR] **Option 1**: Invest €1 million (approximately US$1.15 million) in a qualifying enterprise or project, and hold the investment for three years.
[s059|#bo-qua-so: số VND chỉ nhắc lại số EUR] **Option 2**: Make an endowment of €400,000 (approximately US$459,000) to a social project in the arts, culture, sport or education.
[s060] Please note: the Ireland IIP stopped accepting new applications on February 15, 2023. IMM Group holds a limited number of eligible endowment places from projects that met the legal criteria before that date.
[s061] Ireland golden visa process, step by step
[s062] Deposit and due diligence
[s063] Sign the deposit agreement and complete the initial due diligence on your file.
[s064] Sign the contracts
[s065] Complete the IMM Group service agreement together with the contracts that establish and implement the investment.
[s066] Prepare and file your application
[s067] Prepare the legal documentation and file your application for Irish residency under the Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP).
[s068] Receive pre-approval
[s069] The Irish government reviews the application and issues pre-approval.
[s070] Transfer your investment
[s071] Transfer the investment capital once the application has received pre-approval.
[s072] Travel to Ireland to complete the formalities
[s073] Travel to Ireland to complete the remaining formalities and provide your fingerprints. You receive your residence permit (Stamp 4) during the same trip.
