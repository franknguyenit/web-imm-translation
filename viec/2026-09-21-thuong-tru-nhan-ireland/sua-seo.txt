[s011|#bo-qua-so: nêu lại mốc 15/02/2023 của s004 và ngưỡng €400,000 của s059 ở khối mở đầu] The Irish government's Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP), widely known as the Ireland golden visa, granted Stamp 4 residency to non-EU investors and their families. It closed to new applications on February 15, 2023. A limited number of €400,000 endowment allocations remain available through legally eligible projects.
