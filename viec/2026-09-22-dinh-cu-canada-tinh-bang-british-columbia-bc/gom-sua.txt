[s004] A Canadian immigration route under the British Columbia Provincial Nominee Program (BC PNP) for business owners and senior managers who want to invest in, start or acquire a business in British Columbia.
[s005] You must operate the business in British Columbia yourself to qualify for a provincial nomination and move toward Canadian permanent residence.
[s007] The minimum investment is C$200,000.
[s008] British Columbia offers a milder climate than most of Canada, a location well suited to trade with the U.S., and large, well-established immigrant communities.
[s012] You, your spouse and your dependent children can live, study and work in British Columbia while you hold your work permit.
[s016] Once you have met your investment, business-operating and residency commitments to the province, your family can apply for Canadian permanent residence.
[s021] BC is one of Canada's most dynamic economies and is well positioned for business and international trade.
[s025] The province does not require you to place a deposit to take part in the program.
[s027] This applies to the BC PNP Base Stream, with the investment made in an eligible business.
[s028] No immediate profitability requirement
[s029] The business must be actively operating and create the jobs you committed to; profitability depends on your business model and the market.
[s030] Only 1 new job required
[s034] At least 3 years of experience as a business owner holding 10% or more of the equity within the past 10 years, or at least 4 years of senior management experience within the past 10 years.
[s036] At least 1 year of post-secondary education, or experience owning and running a business in which you held 100% of the equity, as set out in the program guide.
[s038] Clear security, background and medical checks.
[s043] Hands-on, day-to-day management of the business in BC.
[s046] Assessment and case preparation
[s047] We assess your eligibility, your assets, your management experience, your English-language ability and your overall fit with the program.
[s049] You travel to BC to assess the business environment, the market and the investment opportunities in the province.
[s050] Submit your registration (EOI)
[s051] You submit an online registration, the BC PNP's expression of interest step, to the Entrepreneur Immigration program.
[s052] Receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) and submit your application
[s053] Once you are invited to apply, you submit your full application to the province.
[s055] If the province requests one, you attend an interview with the BC PNP.
[s057] Once your application is approved, you sign the performance agreement with the province and apply for a work permit.
[s058] Move to BC to invest and run your business
[s059] You receive your work permit, move to BC, make your investment, operate the business and deliver on your commitments to the province.
[s061] Once you have met the investment, operating and residency conditions, you submit your final report and request the provincial nomination.
