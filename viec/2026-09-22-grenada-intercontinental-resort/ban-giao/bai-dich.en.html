Holding a real estate share in Grenada can be more than an investment intended to generate income and a store of value held in US dollars. A share in InterContinental Grenada Resort — a project approved by the Government of Grenada — also opens a route to citizenship for life for your whole family, with no residence requirement.

Your route to Grenadian citizenship

A qualifying project

If you invest in a qualifying project, you can apply for Grenadian citizenship under Grenada's Citizenship by Investment program.

Citizenship for life

Grenadian citizenship is granted for life if the Government of Grenada approves your application.

The whole family

Your family can be included in the same application, within the range of dependents the program allows at the time you apply.

Dual or multiple citizenship

Grenada allows dual or multiple citizenship, so you can keep your current citizenship if the laws of that country allow it.

What Grenadian citizenship gives you

  • You and your family can receive Grenadian citizenship and passports if your application is approved.
  • The Grenadian passport offers convenient travel access to more than 140 countries and territories, including much of Europe, the United Kingdom, China, and Hong Kong.
  • There is no requirement to live in Grenada to keep your citizenship.
  • The whole process can be completed from your country of residence.

account_balance

02

Basic eligibility

  • The main applicant must be 18 or older.
  • You must be in good health, have no criminal record, and have enough lawfully sourced funds to make the investment.
  • The application goes through due diligence and background checks and is assessed by the Government of Grenada.

03

Projected returns

  • The projected return is about 4% a year over 5 years — roughly US$54,000 in total.
  • Under the project terms, the return is paid after your citizenship application is approved and you have completed 100% of the investment.
  • This is a projected return based on the project documents and contract; actual results depend on construction progress, the resort's operating performance, market conditions, and the terms you sign.

Resort benefits

  • Investors receive 7 complimentary nights a year at the resort for 5 years, as set out in the project documents.
