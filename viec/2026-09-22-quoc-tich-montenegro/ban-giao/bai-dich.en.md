# Montenegro Citizenship by Investment

### What is Montenegro citizenship by investment?

- The Montenegro citizenship by investment program grants citizenship of Montenegro — a European country and NATO member — to non-EU investors and their families.
- You qualify through a government contribution combined with an investment in a designated real estate project.
- If your application is approved, you and your family hold Montenegrin citizenship for life.
- Montenegrin citizenship opens a route to living in the EU if and when Montenegro joins the European Union.
- Once you hold citizenship and have lived in Montenegro for at least 3 continuous years, you may qualify to apply for a US E-2 treaty investor visa.
- **Who you can include:** your spouse, children under 18, and children over 18 who are unmarried and financially dependent.

## Benefits of Montenegrin citizenship

### Visa-free travel to 123 countries

- Visa-free access to 123 countries, including EU member states.

### Healthcare and education

- Access to healthcare and education on the same terms as Montenegrin nationals.

### A route to the US E-2 treaty investor visa

- You may qualify to apply for a US E-2 visa after at least 3 continuous years of residence in Montenegro.

### What EU accession would mean

- If Montenegro joins the EU, investors may gain the right to live and work in EU member states under the rules in force at that time.

## Advantages of the Montenegro citizenship program

### Fast processing

- Citizenship for the whole family has typically been granted in about 6–7 months from filing.

### Lower cost than comparable programs

- One of the lower-cost European citizenship routes, compared with €690,000–€2 million for comparable programs.

### Citizenship for life

- Once granted, citizenship is held for life, with no ongoing requirements to maintain it.

### No property holding period

- There is no minimum holding period for the property; you may sell once the process is complete and you have met your commitments to the developer.

### Government-approved projects

- Only government-approved projects qualify, and the developer places a deposit with the government.

### Keep your current citizenship

- You and your family can keep your current citizenship if your home country permits dual citizenship.

### Apply from your home country

- The whole process can be handled from your country of residence; you do not need to travel to Montenegro before formal approval.

### No Montenegrin tax on foreign income

- Income arising outside Montenegro is not taxed in Montenegro on the basis of citizenship alone; how your income is taxed depends on where you are tax resident, so confirm your own position with an independent tax advisor.

### Simple eligibility requirements

- No language, education, or management experience requirements.

### Connected to Europe

- Montenegro is a NATO member and well placed for travel to Europe and beyond; any right to live in the EU would depend on if and when Montenegro joins.

## Montenegro citizenship by investment requirements

### Applicant requirements

- Aged 18 or older.
- No upper age limit.
- Not a citizen of an EU member state.
- A documented, lawful source of funds.
- No criminal record.

### Investment requirements

- A government contribution of €200,000.
- Combined with an investment in a government-designated property project.
- Property investment: €250,000 in the northern region or €450,000 in the southern region.

### Dependents you can include

- Your legal spouse.
- Children under 18.
- Children over 18 who are still financially dependent and unmarried.

### Documentation requirements

- The applicant and dependents must pass due diligence and medical checks.

## The Montenegro citizenship application process

### Due diligence and filing

We prepare your file, carry out initial due diligence, and submit it together with the fees set by the program.

### Pre-approval and investment

Your file is assessed and your background verified through an authorized agent or government body. Once you are approved, you make the government contribution and the required property investment.

### Naturalization and passport

You travel to Montenegro for biometrics and file your naturalization application, then receive your certificate of naturalization and your passport. Note: processing typically takes around 6–9 months, depending on your file and the reviewing authority.

## Montenegro citizenship by investment FAQs

#### What are the investment risks?

The two main risks

- Choosing a developer or project without a track record, or one that does not qualify under the program.
- Investment funds from an unlawful source, which leads to the application being refused.
- IMM Group reviews your file and the project in detail before any contract is signed, to limit these risks.

Note: real estate is an investment and carries risk.

#### Where can the investment funds come from?

Source of funds requirements

- Documenting your source of funds is generally simpler than it is for programs in the United States, Australia, or Canada.
- Proof works on 2 levels: the source of the funds you are investing, and the source of the wealth behind them.
- Every family documents this differently — contact IMM Group for advice on your own situation.

#### How is the investment structured?

Investment structure

- From €450,000 in total: a €200,000 government contribution plus property from €250,000 (north) or from €450,000 (south).
- Processing typically takes around 6–9 months; the family includes your spouse and unmarried, financially dependent children up to age 28.

#### How long must the property be held?

Property holding period

- The program sets no minimum holding period for the property, and citizenship is held for life once granted.
- You may transfer the property once the process is complete and your commitments to the developer are met, subject to the project terms.
- IMM Group and the developer can help with rental and property management.

#### What happens to your citizenship if the project fails?

How project risk affects citizenship

- Under the structure of the program, citizenship is tied to an eligible application and a completed investment; construction risk is mitigated by the deposit the developer places with the government.
- Even so, this remains a real estate investment that carries risk — IMM Group reviews each project closely before recommending it.

#### Is there a cap on applications?

Program status

- The program was capped by total number of applications and ran for a fixed period; it has been closed to new applications since the end of 2022.
- Contact IMM Group for the current status and the alternatives available. (Updated: June 2026.)

#### What benefits do Montenegrin citizens have, and where can they travel visa-free?

Rights of Montenegrin citizens

- Montenegrin citizens have access to public education and healthcare: children attend public schools free of charge through the end of secondary school, and health insurance covers treatment at public hospitals.
- The Montenegrin passport ranks among the stronger ones, with visa-free or eVisa access to a wide range of countries (the number changes over time).

#### Has Montenegro joined the EU?

EU accession process

- Montenegro is not yet an EU member. Accession talks are under way and no date has been set; previously announced target dates have already shifted.
- The right to live and work across the EU would arise only if and when Montenegro formally joins. (Updated: June 2026.)

#### Is there a US E-2 treaty with Montenegro?

Why Montenegrin citizens can consider the E-2 visa

- The treaty of commerce and navigation between the United States and Montenegro is the basis on which Montenegrin citizens can be considered for the US E-2 treaty investor visa.
- Once you hold citizenship and have 3 or more continuous years of residence, you can be assessed against the E-2 rules in force when you apply. Any changes would depend on the policies of both countries.

#### Is there a residence requirement?

Residence requirements

- The program sets no residence requirement for obtaining or keeping citizenship.
- Note: if you plan to apply for a US E-2 visa later, the E-2 rules set their own requirement for continuous residence in Montenegro.

#### What language is spoken in Montenegro?

Language and language requirements

- The official language is Montenegrin, and English is widely spoken.
- The citizenship by investment application has no language requirement.

#### What are the requirements for the main applicant?

Basic conditions

- The program sets no upper age limit for the applicant; you need only be 18 or older.
- No qualifications, education, language, or management experience are required.

#### Can parents be included?

Who counts as a dependent

- Unmarried, financially dependent children up to age 28 can be included in the application.
- Parents cannot be included in the same application.
- If you need a route that covers parents as well, you could look at another program — for example the Portugal Golden Visa, which covers several generations — or a separate Montenegro application for them.
