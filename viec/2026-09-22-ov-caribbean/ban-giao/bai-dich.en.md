You invest for a residence permit in Curacao — part of the Kingdom of the Netherlands — with a route to Dutch, and therefore EU, citizenship after 5 years.

The route

Dutch citizenship after 5 years → full EU citizenship rights.

Investment routes (choose 1)

- Real estate in Curacao
- A business, or shares in a local company
- A company listed on the Dutch Caribbean Securities Exchange (DCSX)
- A combination of the above

Key benefits

The whole family can live in and travel to Curacao

Visa-free entry to the Netherlands

After 5 years → a route to Dutch citizenship; with a Dutch passport, you can live anywhere in the 27 EU countries

The Dutch passport ranks in the world's top 4 — visa-free access to 188 countries

Advantages

Just 1 day a year in the country keeps the permit valid

Processing in 3–6 months; a choice of investment routes

Points to consider

You receive a residence permit, not citizenship; visa-free entry applies to the Netherlands only

Dutch citizenship requires 9 months a year of residence over 5 years, plus a Dutch language exam

Travel connections from Asia are limited; permanent residence costs far more, at US$838,000
