## Your route to permanent residence in Cyprus

### A qualifying real estate purchase

A qualifying property starts at €300,000 plus VAT and must be bought new from the developer.

### Prepare the file

The application can include you, your spouse, and your dependent children. Besides owning the property, you need to show a secured annual income.

### Become a permanent resident

You need to visit Cyprus once every 2 years to keep the status.

Cyprus permanent residence is not Cypriot citizenship, and it does not give the right to live or work across the whole EU.

### Points to note about permanent residence

- Your right to work and do business, and the tax treatment that applies, must be assessed case by case.
- Cyprus permanent residence is not Cypriot citizenship.
- The right to live and work in other EU countries arises only if you hold EU citizenship or a valid residence permit or work permit in that country.

### The route to Cypriot citizenship

#### Hold permanent residence (PR)

The physical permanent residence card is renewed every 5 years, provided you still own the property.

#### Meet the residence and language conditions

At least 8 years of physical residence within an 11-year period, Greek language at CEFR level B1, and a pass in the cultural integration test.

#### Cypriot (EU) citizenship

Dual citizenship is allowed. A Cypriot passport gives visa-free travel to more than 170 destinations.
