[s063] What counts as passive income?
[s064] What counts as passive income
[s065] It is steady income that does not come from employment in Portugal: rental income, dividends, returns on financial investments, a pension, and similar. The income has to arise outside Portugal and meet the minimum threshold set by the rules. Every file documents this differently — contact IMM Group for advice on your own situation.

[s068] The applicant documents passive income from €10,440 a year, plus €5,220 a year for each additional adult and €3,132 a year for each dependent child. These figures are indicative at the time of writing and can change with the rules.
